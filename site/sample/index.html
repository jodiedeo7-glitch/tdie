Services

Services for attorneys

Four services, each scoped to the case and the records involved. Timelines are agreed per engagement.

Preliminary case screening

Purpose
Identify the clinical issues in a set of records that may warrant further review, and note gaps in what was produced.
Deliverable
A short written summary of clinical issues observed, questions raised, and records to request.
Typical stage
Before a case is accepted or early in investigation. A screening informs your decision; it doesn't establish merit.
Ask about a screening

Medical chronologies

Purpose
Organize the records into one sequence so events, orders, and responses can be followed in time.
Deliverable
A table of dated entries with source references, with reviewer observations kept in a separate column.
Typical stage
After records are produced, and before expert review or depositions.
Ask about a chronology

Nursing standard-of-care review

Purpose
Compare documented nursing care with the facility policies, protocols, and published guidelines that applied at the time.
Deliverable
A written review identifying where documented care aligns with or departs from those references, for attorney and expert evaluation.
Typical stage
Once the chronology is in place and the relevant policies are available.
Ask about a review

Deposition and discovery preparation

Purpose
Help attorneys prepare to question clinical witnesses and identify what else to request.
Deliverable
Suggested topics and questions, a list of additional records to request, and a glossary of clinical terms in the file.
Typical stage
During discovery, ahead of depositions of nurses or other clinical staff.
Ask about preparation support

Services support attorneys and their retained experts. They are not legal advice or expert testimony.

About

Avery Collins, BSN, RN, LNC

Fourteen years at the bedside

Avery spent fourteen years as a registered nurse in intensive care and emergency departments. That work meant charting under pressure, reading other clinicians' notes during handoffs, and knowing where in a chart to look when something didn't add up.

Bringing that experience to legal work

Avery completed legal nurse consulting training to bring that clinical background to record review for attorneys. The consulting work focuses on the four services listed on the Services page: screening, chronologies, nursing standard-of-care review, and deposition and discovery preparation.
