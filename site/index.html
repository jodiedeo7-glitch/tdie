Questions

Before you book

Anything else? Email jodie.deo7@gmail.com.

I don't have my first case yet. Is it too early?

Not at all. Most new consultants want their website ready before they start reaching out, so attorneys who look you up find something professional from day one. The studio builds your website and the listed materials; it can't promise cases or inquiries.

I don't have a logo or a headshot.

You can still book. A logo isn't required; your business name can be set in type. If you don't have a headshot yet, say so in your intake so the About page can be planned around it.

How do I choose my design?

Browse the signature looks. After you book, your intake form asks which one feels closest, how you want attorneys to see you, and the colors you love or want to avoid. Can't decide? Say so on the form and I'll recommend one.

What will the intake form ask?
  • Your name, credentials, and licensed states
  • Your clinical background: specialties, settings, years
  • Any LNC training or certification
  • The services you offer and the case types you know best
  • Plaintiff attorneys, defense attorneys, or both
  • Links to your headshot and logo, if you have them
  • Your domain, if you've bought one, and sites you like
  • Your style preferences: look, colors, and photo

Already booked? Open the intake form.

Who owns my site and domain?

You do. Your domain stays in your own account with the company you bought it from (like Porkbun or GoDaddy). Your site is hosted on a free plan, you get a copy of your site files at launch, and it can be moved into a hosting account in your name at no charge.

Are there ongoing costs?

Hosting is free. Your only required ongoing cost is your domain's yearly renewal, plus an email subscription if you choose one. The Website Care Plan is optional.

Can I edit my site after launch?

For 14 days, anything that doesn't work as delivered is fixed free. Sites are hand-coded, so there's no drag-and-drop editor; updates are made for you through the Website Care Plan or quoted separately.

I'm rebuilding. What changes?

You keep your domain, and only its website settings change, so your business email keeps working. You choose which text, photos, and logo to carry over. Old pages beyond the new four, blogs, and stores aren't carried over, and current search rankings can't be guaranteed. You're never asked for passwords.

Will the prices go up?

Yes. The first 10 clients get founding rates: $497 for the website and $697 for the Complete LNC Launch. After that, they go to $797 and $997.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are non-refundable once your intake form has been submitted.

Will my site be shown as a before-and-after?

Only if you say yes. The intake form asks for your permission, and saying no doesn't change your price or your package.
