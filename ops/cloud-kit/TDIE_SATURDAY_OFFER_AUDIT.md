## THE FORMAT
Runs every Saturday, 3:00 PM Value slot, category ✨Build & Ship✨, open all weekend. Not a selling post: no tier, no price in the body. The ask is always one sentence. Jodie replies to every one with the actual rewritten sentence.

## HOW JODIE ANSWERS
1. Name the one thing she got right.
2. Name the failure mode in plain words.
3. Write the rewritten sentence in full, on its own line.
Then one line: put it in your shop bio, link in bio and the first line of your sales page.

Five failure modes: sells the container, not the contents · buries the surprising part · lists what's inside instead of what it saves her · names the person and stops there · starts the sentence with yourself.

Formula: who she is → what she walks away with → what it costs her. Format, price and bonuses go underneath.

Four checks: does it start with her or with you · does it name a length or a format · does it contain a price · could she repeat it to a friend from memory.

## THE PROMPT ROTATION (12 weeks, then cycles)
1. One sentence describing what you sell. No feature list.
2. Finish this: "I help ___ get ___ without ___."
3. Say what you sell the way you'd say it to a friend at school pickup.
4. Your offer has one genuinely surprising thing in it. Post only that.
5. One sentence that's true even if the buyer has never heard of you.
6. What does she stop doing once she buys? Don't mention the product.
7. The one line you'd put on a business card with no logo and no job title.
8. One sentence, and the word "and" is banned.
9. What's the before, and what's the after? Two short lines.
10. Say what you sell without naming the format.
11. One sentence that makes the wrong person walk away.
12. Post your current shop bio exactly as it's live. We fix it in public.

## RULES ON MEMBER WORDS
No names, no screenshots, no tagging. Quote as anonymous sentences. Soften identifying details. Never frame a member's price as a TDIE price.
