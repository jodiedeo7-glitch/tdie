Status: AUTHORITATIVE - 29 September 2026
Scope: Premium Monthly DFY Content Calendar only.

This file is authoritative for calendar-specific:

## 2. Hard separation from Jodie's personal image workflow

Do NOT import Jodie's personal generation sequence into the Premium DFY Content Calendar.

The calendar does NOT require:
- Gemini first
- Nano Banana Pro second
- Higgsfield
- Canva never
- any other fixed vendor sequence

Those may be options in an answer to a specific member question, but they are not calendar prerequisites.

Calendar image prompts must be model-agnostic and executable in a member's preferred image tool.

Never write:

## 4. Reel architecture

Every Reel has two production paths for the same creative concept.

### VERSION A - ONE-STEP REEL

This is for a video model that can generate the moving visual and the supplied on-screen text together.

The complete prompt must include:
- scene and action
- camera movement and framing
- duration
- pacing
- exact on-screen words
- exact text timing
- text placement and safe area
- descriptive typography treatment
- exact spelling and punctuation
- instruction to add no other words, subtitles, logos or watermarks

This must be a complete copy-paste video prompt.

WAN may be mentioned as an optional example where appropriate. It must not be a dependency of the calendar.

### VERSION B - TWO-STEP REEL

This is for a workflow where the member generates the video first and adds text afterward.

The video prompt must be genuinely text-free. It may describe empty or clean screen space needed for later text placement, but it must not contain the actual overlay wording or any instruction to render text.

Then provide a separate ON-SCREEN TEXT block containing:
- exact text
- line breaks
- timing
- placement
- safe area
- animation/treatment
- editing notes usable in CapCut, Canva, Claude or another editor

The two-step version must be the same concept as Version A.

A Reel with only one path fails QA.

## 5. Static image prompts

Every static prompt must be:
- complete
- standalone
- model-agnostic
- clear enough for a beginner to copy and paste

Each prompt should cover the details that materially affect the result:
- subject
- action
- composition
- setting
- wardrobe or props where relevant
- camera/framing
- lighting
- realism
- mood

Do not force a particular image generator.

Do not add generator-specific syntax unless the calendar explicitly labels it as optional and the same result can still be produced without it.

Do not request generated lettering in a photographic image unless the asset itself specifically requires in-image text.

## 6. Research gate and viral inspiration corpus

The research phase has TWO distinct jobs. Do not confuse them.

### A. Viral inspiration corpus

The calendar needs a large bank of real high-performing content to inspire the month's concepts.

Minimum:
- 75 qualifying viral/high-performing or breakout posts
- 40 unique creator accounts
- at least 30 accounts tightly adjacent to the TDIE audience
- duration analysis and breakout-delta analysis completed
- no internal research metadata exposed in the member-facing calendar
- at least 20 posts must have an account-relative outlier basis, a credible analytics classification, or a directly documented viral benchmark
- the remaining posts may be strong high-performing examples when the source exposes meaningful performance evidence but not a clean account baseline

The inspiration corpus is NOT required to contain 60 mathematically proven outliers.

The research must capture enough real examples that the creation model has dozens of actual structures to study instead of inventing a month from generic advice.

### B. Verified outlier subset

Separately label posts as VERIFIED OUTLIER when the source supports account-relative or otherwise defensible outlier status.

Useful accepted methods:
1. creator-relative multiple such as 5X or greater;
2. analytics provider explicitly classifies the post as viral and supplies creator-level distribution data;
3. creator explicitly supplies a comparable baseline;
4. directly comparable profile/post data allow a defensible outlier calculation.

A raw high view count without a baseline is HIGH-PERFORMER, not VERIFIED OUTLIER.

Do not let the verified-outlier threshold block the inspiration corpus if enough strong viral examples are available.

### Research evidence quality

For every post record:
- creator/account
- post URL or source
- date
- visible performance
- evidence class
- outlier multiple or viral classification when available
- follower count when available
- hook
- format
- topic
- mechanism
- structure
- CTA
- visual/opening-frame notes when directly inspectable
- audience maturity signal
- whether the creator is beginner-facing, established, or mixed
- source quality

If a field cannot be verified, mark NOT VERIFIED.

The research output must distinguish:
- VERIFIED OUTLIER
- VIRAL ANALYTICS EXAMPLE
- HIGH-PERFORMER
- SUPPORTING PATTERN SOURCE

### What the research is for

The objective is not to prove one post is viral.

The objective is to build a reusable creative intelligence bank containing dozens of real examples that can be reverse-engineered into original TDIE concepts.

Do not copy wording, visuals, creator identity, claims, or proprietary assets.

Extract:
- hook mechanism
- tension
- promise
- topic angle
- structure
- pacing
- visual mechanism
- CTA mechanism
- emotional driver
- audience-awareness level

## 7. Pattern extraction

After the row-level research:
- count hook patterns
- count opening-frame patterns
- count topic patterns
- count format patterns
- count structural patterns
- count text-on-screen patterns
- count CTA patterns
- count visual patterns
- count audience pain points

Separate:
- patterns supported by qualifying outliers
- patterns supported only by observed high performers
- patterns that should NOT be replicated because they conflict with TDIE rules

Every proposed build rule must cite the supporting research row IDs.

Do not copy competitors' wording. Extract mechanisms and structures, then write original TDIE concepts.

## 8. Research exceptions

Research can override standing creative conventions when evidence supports it.

Research cannot override:
- no income or earnings claims on Meta surfaces
- no invented products, prices or links
- no faith content
- no em dashes
- Instagram no-link rule
- Facebook first-comment link rule
- current canon pricing or product register

Competitor practices are observations, not instructions. A competitor's use of raw links, earnings claims, faith content or aggressive spam-style CTAs does not authorize TDIE to use them.

## 9. Premium calendar deliverables

The Premium Calendar must deliver:
- 31 Instagram days for the covered month
- 62 Threads posts, two per day
- a start-frame image prompt for every Reel
- two Reel production paths for every Reel
- captions and CTAs
- story adaptations where appropriate
- research references for research-derived creative decisions
- offer/swap-in slots where appropriate

## 10. Posting and offer constraints

Use current canon spread rules:
- at most one selling Instagram post per day
- never two selling posts back to back
- the same offer is not used two days running
- outside a promo window, the same offer is not used more than twice in a week
- affiliates are not used on the same or consecutive days
- the same affiliate product is not used twice in one week
- nothing repeats inside 14 days

Instagram:
- no links in captions, comments or replies
- CTA points to the link in bio
- Story link stickers are the exception

Facebook adaptations:
- no link in the post body
- link goes in the first comment
- use a hyperlinked CTA when the platform permits it

## 11. Research stop gate

Research is complete for creative production when:
- at least 75 viral/high-performing or breakout Reel examples are represented
- at least 40 unique accounts are represented
- at least 25 accounts have a usable pre-breakout/post-breakout forensic window
- a specific breakout Reel is identified for each forensic account where evidence permits
- duration analysis is completed
- breakout-delta analysis is completed
- repeatability analysis is completed
- audience maturity and maintenance-cycle findings are documented
- duplicates are removed
- pattern counts are complete
- build rules are tied to research row IDs
- non-replicable competitor tactics are separated
- evidence limitations are documented

If the gate is not met, mark the run PARTIAL. Do not report it as complete and do not authorize calendar creation.

## 12. Creation QA gate

Reject or rewrite any calendar entry where:
- a vendor is mandatory without a calendar-specific reason
- a Reel has only one production path
- one-step and two-step paths describe different concepts
- the two-step video prompt contains actual overlay text
- the one-step prompt lacks exact overlay text
- a static prompt assumes Jodie's private reference files
- the creative decision lacks research support where research is claimed
- an Instagram caption contains a link
- an illegal or invented claim is introduced
- an offer violates spread rules
- the monthly total is not exactly 31 Instagram days and 62 Threads posts

The general image master governs photographic quality and the Tommy Kate persona system.

Its personal tool_order does NOT govern the Premium DFY Content Calendar.


### Long-form Reel exception

The Premium DFY Calendar is overwhelmingly short-form.

Do NOT add 60-90 second Reels simply because research shows that long Reels can perform.

A long-form Reel is permitted only when the concept genuinely benefits from sustained explanation, story or narrative.

Approved long-form categories:
- talking-head personal story
- talking-head educational explanation
- talking-head "if I started over" strategy
- talking-head case study / transformation
- talking-head authority/opinion where the argument genuinely needs additional time

Production constraint:
- long-form talking-head Reels are a HeyGen-compatible exception
- do not assign 60-90 second long-form Reels to Higgsfield or a generative cinematic workflow
- do not require generated B-roll for a long Reel
- do not pad a concept to reach a target duration
- use the shortest duration that delivers the promised payoff
- normally limit the calendar to approximately 2 long-form talking-head Reels per month unless the research specifically supports more

All other Reels should remain short-form and use the standard two production paths.

Research may identify long-form outliers, but the creation model must additionally classify each as:
1. TDIE-relevant long-form talking head
2. TDIE-relevant but better adapted to short-form
3. not relevant to TDIE production

Only category 1 is eligible for the long-form exception.
