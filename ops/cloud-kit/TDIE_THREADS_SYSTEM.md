## 1a · THE FIVE-LAYER SALES ARCHITECTURE (Kira, Module 3)
- **Layer 1, INTERRUPT (Identity/Belief):** reach engine. Short declarations that make the reader feel personally addressed. No product, no link. Formula: [who you're talking to]: [truth, challenge or declaration].
- **Layer 2, PROVE (Proof):** specific number, timeframe, product name. Lead with the number.
- **Layer 3, TEACH (Long Form Teaching):** insight, system, result. Numbered lists are Kira's best shape. Lives in 11 AM and 3 PM.
- **Layer 4, ASPIRE (Lifestyle):** shows the life, not the product. For Jodie: real founder life, an actual win or an actual mess, never staged.
- **Layer 5, CONVERT (Soft sell):** product as the punchline of a story, never the headline. "You never hard pitch. Ever." The 5 PM slot.
Other named types (from "The Types of Threads List"): Good Morning, Inspiring Good Morning, Viral Optimized (built only to get seen; Unpopular Opinion, Bold Claim + Evidence, List of Truths), Let's Connect ("If you are [ideal buyer] who is [trying to do X] but [obstacle]… you are exactly who I built this for."), Question Thread (Be honest… / Girls don't gatekeep… / Serious question…), SPOILER, Goodnight, Standalone.
