- This file replaces every earlier version of itself. Nothing older survives underneath.
- Look rules written in any other file (AGENTS.md, ops/canon/TDIE_CANON.md section 8, ops/canon/canon.json "look", README_START_HERE.md, the 4 October handoff rules) are retired. Where they disagree with this file, this file wins.
- When Jodie gives a new look rule, REPLACE the old line here. Never stack a dated note on top of an old one. One rule, one line, one place.
- These are hard limits, not a layout brief. Do not turn them into a template. Jodie dislikes anything that "looks AI".
- Photo and image-prompt rules (Tommy Kate, generators, the tumbler) are not here. They live in TDIE_IMAGE_GENERATION_MASTER.md.

## The look
Scrapbook glam: loud, pink, sparkly, confident, girly, grown-up and premium. Layered and textured: torn paper, real Polaroids with glitter tape, notebook-paper cards, contrast borders, shadow boxes, detail. NOTHING CHILDISH. Never minimalist, beige, neutral or quiet.

Reference images Jodie shares are for feel and layout only. Never take their colours. Never copy them.

## Colours
- Hot pink #D62E73 (deep #B8245F)
- Bubblegum #E85A9B
- Lavender #E9DCFF (allowed as a background)
- Pink #FFD3E6, light pink #FFF0F6
- Text near-black #1A1417. Never grey body text.
- Cream is allowed, as long as it is not flat.
- Gold appears ONLY as the quote marks on review speech bubbles. Nowhere else.
- Never plum, magenta, chocolate, brown or black fills, boxes or buttons.
- The colour variables already in the site's code stay as they are. Do not recolour existing pages to match this list.

## Fonts
- Gloock for headlines, upright, never italic.
- Jost for everything else: body, prices, labels, buttons.
- Yellowtail script ONLY on the stressed word or words in a headline. Never on every pink word.
- Caveat only as the photograph-caption font.
- Never Newsreader, Inter, Fraunces, Cormorant Garamond or Montserrat.

## Backgrounds
- Never flat, plain or boring.
- Never one colour from the top of the page to the bottom.
- No flat cream, white or soft blush.
- No grid paper.
- No alternating flat pink and lavender sections.
- No pink and lavender gradient or ombre washes.
- Sections are clearly separated, but the separations are not oversized.

## Glitter
- NEVER a full background. Not the page, not one section.
- Accent only: borders, glitter tape, loose scattered pieces.
- Background glitter is SILVER, not hot pink.
- The glitter tape stays exactly as it is. Do not lighten or restyle it.
- No glitter triangles. No blurry "spray paint" glitter circles.

## Real textures, never drawn in code
Glitter, torn paper, tape and Polaroid frames must be real image files. Never fake them with code-drawn shapes, gradients or sparkle. Real files already on the site: `public/images/brand/silver-glitter-tile.webp`, `sparkle-scatter.webp`, `sparkle-glow.webp`. If a texture is missing, get a real one (Canva elements first) and show Jodie before using it.

## Photos and frames
- Frames match the photo's shape. Never crop a portrait photo into a square.
- Polaroids are shaped like real Polaroids: thin even top and sides, deep bottom strip.
- Polaroid sets keep the stacked look (a sheet behind each). Frames side by side never touch.
- No white text box or label over pin photos. No opaque boxes over her photos.
- Tommy Kate photos are fine on the site. Just never a random one as the $97 course hero.
- Page images look premium and show the actual product or service, not unrelated lifestyle photos.

## Pages
- Pages share the same style, not the same layout.
- The product's name is always shown prominently.
- Every clickable card or image shows its name and a button.
- Price-tag boxes overlapping hero photos are fine.
- Reviews go in glossy pink speech bubbles with gold quote marks, next to the product they are about.
- Every While-You-Sleep page links to the other While-You-Sleep pages.
- The header on the Real Examples page (/shop/while-you-sleep-storefront/real-examples) is the correct site header.
- Buttons: never a filled hot-pink pill.
- Nothing oversized (the sizing rule in AGENTS.md still applies).
- Lists in on-screen text are numbered or dashed, never run together.
- Mockups are shown in place on the full page, under the header and hero.
- Copy should make the reader think "I NEED THIS". No hype, no income claims.

## Rejected. Never again.
Bows. Painted florals, roses, gold swirls, gold foil. Holographic foil, pearls, sequins, satin, ribbons, envelopes. Bubble letters. Bubbly holographic icons: glossy hearts, gem pins, glitter stars. Scalloped edges. Clip-art stars. Hydrangeas (lilacs and peonies only). Gingham as a background.

Open it the way she will see it, at full size, on desktop AND phone. Check every section for cut-off text, blank spots, wrong photos, overlaps and tiny text. Fix what you find and look again. Say exactly what you could not check.
