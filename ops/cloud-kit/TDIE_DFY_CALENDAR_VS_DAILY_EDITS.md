UPDATE FROM JODIE, 2 OCTOBER 2026. THIS REPLACES ANYTHING OLDER YOU HAVE ON THESE TWO PRODUCTS.

The Digital Income Edit (TDIE) has two image products. They are completely separate. Never mix their looks, rules, titles or files.

PRODUCT 1: PREMIUM DFY CONTENT CALENDAR (SOP 15)
What it is: 31 days of Instagram content a month for Membership Premium, delivered as one Skool lesson per day in the Monthly DFY Content Calendar module. Each day has a caption and hashtags, a ChatGPT rewrite prompt, how to make the Reel, a cover image prompt, a still frame image prompt, Reel Version A (video with on-screen text), Reel Version B (text-free video) plus its on-screen text block, Threads posts, story slides and notes. It runs live on Tommy Kate's Instagram, @the.faceless.homestead.mama.
The look: TOMMY KATE'S REAL LIFE. Tommy Kate is a real-feeling member of TDIE: a homesteading gamer girl and mom, extremely wealthy and self-made, building a faceless digital business from her farmhouse. Quiet wealth, lived in. Her account must feel like a real person whose life you follow, so every characteristic of her world is repeated, month after month:
- In every frame she appears in (full body, hands only or boots only): her glitter-flecked pink iced coffee tumbler with a lavender straw, saturated candy pink, never pale. It is never the only pink item.
- Her bubblegum pink ceramic mug printed Player Two? in black lettering (never a plain pink mug). Lavender Xbox controller with a strawberry charm, clearly purple-toned. Pink and lavender GameBoys, visibly worn. Hello Kitty and Kuromi plushies, which live in her pink attic loft and are in every frame of the loft. Mason jar of wildflowers (named bouquets are lilacs or peonies). Worn brown leather farm boots with dried mud in the welt. Scrunchie on the wrist, gold claw clip, fine gold chain with a small heart pendant. Pink gingham bedding. Candy-pink over-ear headphones, candy-pink gardening gloves, candy-pink velvet throw pillow. Her golden retriever and its rope toy. Her chickens and ducks.
- Her places: the pink attic loft (ivy-wrapped beams, plush pink shag rug, pastel pink gaming desk, glass-panel PC glowing pink, the plushies, the gingham bed); the farmhouse kitchen (blush cabinets, butcher-block island, copper pans with patina); the porch (weathered rail, chipped paint, folded quilt, potted herbs, rocking chair); the living room; the pasture, split rail fencing, red barn, wildflower fields, chicken coop and raised garden beds.
- Her wardrobe: oversized pink knits, soft tees, hoodies, sweatpants, leggings, fuzzy socks, ribbed tanks, high-waisted vintage denim, barn coat, worn boots, in blush, cream, sage, rust, lavender, oatmeal and candy pink. Never blouses, never blazers, never costume cottagecore.
- Each frame with her carries the tumbler plus two or three of these named items, written in the first half of the prompt. Every room appears at least once a week and every named item at least once a month. Frames without her carry exactly one saturated candy-pink object, rotated.
- Captions and on-screen words are in Tommy Kate's voice. No links on Instagram ever; calls to action point to the link in bio. No income or earnings claims on Instagram or Facebook. No em dashes.
- Lesson titles: Day N - Reel · [the post's hook], plain words, under 50 characters.

PRODUCT 2: DAILY EDITS (SOP 16)
What it is: one free photograph prompt a day in the Skool Daily Prompts course, open to every member, with the finished photo at the top of the lesson. No captions, no hashtags, no video. Never called a calendar.
The look: THE UNIVERSAL LOOK. A member must be able to attach her own seed image and paste the prompt with no rewriting, so it carries a look any woman can step into, not Tommy Kate's specific house and objects. Rotate four lanes: refined homestead (farmhouse kitchen with bread rising, porch at golden hour, orchard, kitchen garden, farmers' market, apron and basket); refined mom life (quiet morning before the house wakes, school-run morning, tidied playroom; children only as evidence like a small backpack or a crayon drawing, never a child in the frame); quiet millionaire girl boss (running her business from a beautiful lived-in home, a calm cafe corner or a window seat, with laptop, notebook, planner, coffee); cozy evenings in (reading nook, blanket, bath, a plain unbranded controller or a book, about one day in seven).
- Wardrobe: fine-gauge knits, cashmere, linen sets, quality tees, tailored trousers, wide-leg denim, slip skirts, trench and wool coats, aprons, clean boots, simple gold jewellery. No logos.
- One saturated candy-pink accent per photo, written as something anyone could own (a pink knit, a pink mug with no words, peonies, a pink notebook).
- Never: anything only Tommy Kate owns (Player Two? mug, glitter tumbler with lavender straw, lavender controller with strawberry charm, GameBoys, Hello Kitty or Kuromi plushies, the pink attic gaming loft, her golden retriever, her name). Never the cookie-cutter rich AI influencer: supercars, Lamborghinis, private jets, yachts, penthouses, Dubai, mansions, marble, hotel suites, champagne towers, designer logos, branded handbags, cash or card props, nightclubs, pool parties, high-fashion editorial posing.
- Eleven blocks per prompt, 2,500 to 3,000 characters, one complete standalone prompt per day, ending with: Cinematic lived-in realism: [three words for the mood].
- Lesson titles: [M]/[D] [Scene Name], two to four evocative words, never a camera setup. Example: 10/14 Orchard Before Breakfast.

RULES FOR BOTH
- Every prompt with a woman in it opens with exactly: "Photograph of this exact woman from the attached reference sheet. Identity is taken only from the reference."
- Never write her locked features in words, in any form: face, facial structure, freckles, skin tone, eye colour, hair colour, hair length and cut, body proportions, age. Not even "same face". The seed image supplies them, which is why any member's seed works and nobody's brown eyes turn blue. How the hair is worn, nails, wardrobe, jewellery, footwear and what she is doing are written freely.
- Jodie always generates with Tommy Kate's seed, so every woman in Jodie's images is Tommy Kate.
- No hex codes in a prompt; colours are named with their shade (saturated candy pink, never pale). No text or logos in a photo unless the prompt is a cover or Reel with deliberate on-screen words. Every image gets its own complete standalone prompt.
- Third-party characters: Tommy Kate's Hello Kitty and Kuromi plushies are allowed and required in her home in calendar photos. Still out everywhere: printed logos, wordmarks and brand names in frame, a character as the subject of a post, any character on a product TDIE sells or in a listing or pin title, branded handbags, film or franchise likenesses.
- Correction: no account has ever been deactivated for imagery. The only deactivation was the Tommy Kate Threads account, caused by several tabs auto-posting to Threads at once. Drop any note you have that says third-party IP caused deactivations.

When I ask for a calendar day, use PRODUCT 1 only. When I ask for a daily prompt or a Daily Edit, use PRODUCT 2 only. If you are not sure which one I mean, ask me that one question before writing.
