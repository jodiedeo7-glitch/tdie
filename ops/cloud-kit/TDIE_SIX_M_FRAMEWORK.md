> Copy of the project doc of the same name, placed here for cloud sessions (26 Sep 2026). Governing conversion framework, adopted 21 Sep 2026: "Work this framework into everything we do for my business." Sits under canon: where it disagrees with `ops/canon/canon.json`, canon wins.
> **Price note for this copy:** the yes-ladder below was written before later price changes. Always use canon.json prices: Membership Premium is quoted at $35/month · $297/year (Decision 114); 8 Claude Prompts is retired and replaced by The Operating Prompts™ ($19) (Decision 102).
> **Meta link rule (Decisions 115 and 117):** no links anywhere on Instagram (CTA points to link in bio). Facebook: no link in the post body, link only in the first comment 5 minutes after posting. Skool links are always hyperlinked CTA words, never a raw URL.

## 1. MAGNET: the first line earns the lean-in
Every asset opens with one of three devices.
- **Self-reference:** "If you're X, this is for you." Specific enough that she says "that's me."
- **True exclusivity**, only when literally true: lessons that live only in the Skool classroom; The Premium Vault (every guide unlocked, Premium only); the systems actually running inside TDIE (Scaling & Systems, Module 9); the prompts Jodie runs the business on. No manufactured scarcity, no fake deadlines, no invented counts.
- **Reciprocity:** give first, with a nameable value. TDIE gifts ($0, all in canon): The Weekend Ecosystem™ preview (no email), The Faceless Income Blueprint™, $10/Day Traffic Machine — Starter Map, FREE GUIDE - One & Done Money Stream, The Weekend Build Challenge, The Shopify Starter Kit, Find Your Door, the day's prompt in the Daily Free Prompt tab.

## 2. MESSAGE: she is the hero, TDIE is the guide
Ideal state first, mechanism second. **Delete test:** delete the product name; if what's left doesn't describe a change in her life (time back, control, not showing her face, a business that sells when she isn't working), rewrite.
- Proof is "show the machine": TDIE was built faceless and solo with AI. Never income screenshots.
- Build order, said the same way everywhere: offer first, funnel second, automation third, audience last.
- Resume copy (module counts, lesson counts) goes below the ideal state, never above.
- On Facebook and Instagram, the ideal state is time, freedom and control, never dollar figures.

Weak: "The Weekend Ecosystem™ is a 22-module course covering offers, funnels, automation and traffic."
Strong: "You want a business that keeps selling on the Tuesday you're too tired to post. That's what gets built inside The Weekend Ecosystem™: offer first, funnel second, automation third, audience last."

## 3. MICRO-COMMITMENT: smallest valuable yes first
Yes-ladder (use canon.json prices): free no-email (Weekend Ecosystem™ preview, /learn library, Find Your Door) → free with email (Blueprint, Starter Map, One & Done guide, Weekend Build Challenge, Shopify Starter Kit) → first purchase (small guides, Membership Standard $9/month · $99/year with 7-day free trial) → core (The Weekend Ecosystem™ $97 or 3 × $33.33) → deeper (Membership Premium $35/month · $297/year, DFY services). After a no, offer the next-smaller yes, never a discount. BAMI and Vault Unlock stay out of every new asset.

## 4. MAKE THE ASK: one clear CTA, said often
1. One ask per asset, repeated; every link appears twice (early and at the close).
2. One unexpected touch in the CTA.
3. Strong verbs: claim, grab, start, take, open, get.
4. Personal: "your", "my".
5. Direct: ask for exactly what you want.
6. Match temperature: cold = one free thing; warm = first purchase or tier; hot (buyer) = next rung.
7. Make it a trade: say what she gets for what she gives.
Link sits on the CTA words, never a raw URL (www.thedigitalincomeedit.com is the only exception). Affiliate link, rel="sponsored" and disclosure ship together.

## 5. MONEY
Owned before affiliate in any paid slot. Every asset routes to something TDIE owns (the MailerLite list or Skool). Pinterest routes to Skool only (domain block). Every push becomes an evergreen asset.

## 6. MAP
Launch cadence: seed at 90 days, announce at 60, one in four posts at 45, every other post at 30, everything in the final 14. Signature hot pink is the activation colour (in generator prompts write "saturated signature hot pink, never pale", never a hex). Tommy Kate carries launches so Jodie never has to appear.

## GUARDRAILS NEVER OVERRIDDEN
No manufactured scarcity. No fabricated proof ("1,200+ women already inside" is true; invented figures are not). No promised timeline for results. No earnings figures on Facebook or Instagram. No third-party IP. Never misstate what a tier includes (The Value Vault is à la carte at Standard; Premium has every guide unlocked). Every product, price, link and CTA comes from canon.json products[] or key_pages.

## THE PRE-SHIP CHECK (every asset)
1. Opens with a Magnet device.
2. Any exclusivity or deadline is literally true.
3. Ideal state before mechanism; passes the delete test.
4. One CTA, sized to the reader's temperature.
5. Strong verb, "your/my", states the trade.
6. Link on the CTA words, appears twice.
7. Routes to something TDIE owns.
8. Every product, price and link is a canon row.
9. On Facebook or Instagram: no earnings figures.
10. Inside a launch window: stage-correct frequency.
11. Passes the real-person voice test.
