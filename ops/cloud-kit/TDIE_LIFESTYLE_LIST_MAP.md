# LIFESTYLE LIST MAP (copy for cloud sessions)

Copy of the project doc `claude/TDIE_LIFESTYLE_LIST_MAP.md` (25 Sep 2026), placed here 26 Sep 2026. The evergreen look bank behind the Legally Blonde Amazon pin factory (`WYS_REFERENCE_PACK_2026-10-07.md` in this folder).

**What this is:** the standing bank of look ideas NOT tied to a holiday. The factory runs at least one evergreen look a week, two outside the Halloween windows. Every holiday look earns for about three weeks and then stops; these lines still sell in March, and they make the storefront read as a shop rather than a costume box (which matters when Amazon reviews the account for tier status and onsite eligibility).

**Rules carried over from the factory recipe:** every look is a flat lay (or shoppable collage) plus a Tommy Kate lifestyle photo, spaced 3 days apart (car and other theme lists are flat lay only). Every look gets an Amazon Idea List, and that list URL is the pin link. No prices anywhere. No third-party IP in any image. No em dashes. Disclosure on every pin.

**Picking:** a line that hasn't run in 30 days; dress it for the current season; never two clothing looks as the evergreen pair in one week (pair one wearable with one space).

Categories (must match the lifestyle JSON `category` field exactly): clothing, accessories, jewelry, beauty, perfume, home-decor, dorm, car, books, gifts. Evergreen pages use `season: "Evergreen"`.
