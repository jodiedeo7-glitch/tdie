## THE LESSON

Say what you sell in one sentence. No feature list. No "and it also comes with." One sentence.

If you can't, that isn't a writing problem. It's the thing quietly costing you every sale, because if you can't say it in one line, she can't repeat it to her friend, and she is never going to buy something she can't explain to herself.

I ran this on 22 women in one weekend. Every one of them failed it the same way.

**They all led with what they made. Not one led with what she gets.**

**A sentence that works has three parts, in this order:**

1. **Who she is**: specific enough that the wrong person self-selects out.
2. **What she walks away with**: the state she's in afterwards, not the thing you hand her.
3. **What it costs her to get there**: time, effort, the thing she doesn't have to do.

The format goes underneath. The price goes underneath. The bonus list goes underneath. Underneath is not nowhere, it's just not first.

**The five ways it goes wrong, with real examples.**

**1 · You sell the container, not the contents.**
> "The $147 Bookkeeping Clarity Session is a personalized 60-minute session for women in business who are tired of guessing about their numbers and ready to understand their finances, identify what needs attention, and walk away with a clear action plan."

Rewrite: **"In 60 minutes you'll stop guessing about your numbers and leave with a plan you can actually follow."**

**2 · You bury the surprising part.**
> "I create Hollywood-level professional headshots remotely from anywhere in the world, personally directing and capturing your images using your smartphone."

Rewrite: **"I direct you through your own phone camera and you walk away with headshots that look like a studio shoot."** Say the surprising part first, explain it second.

**3 · You list what's inside instead of what it saves her.**
> "I created a 1,000 Quote App with photo prompt, hooks, captions, and hashtags. Your all-in-one app for a quote page."

Rewrite: **"Never sit down to write a quote post again, your whole month is already written."**

**4 · You name the person and stop there.**
> "Printable solutions for overwhelmed nurse moms."

Rewrite: **"I help overwhelmed nurse moms get their week back with printables they can use tonight."** Name her, then say what she gets, then say how fast.

**5 · You start the sentence with yourself.**
> "I created [brand] for women who want to turn ordinary Halloween content into bold, luxurious, scroll-stopping visuals without spending hours figuring out what to create."

Rewrite: **"Turn your Halloween content into visuals that look expensive, without spending a night figuring out what to make."** Cut "I created." Start where she is.

### The four checks
- Does it start with her, or with you? If it starts with "I created," "I offer," "I help you get", rewrite it.
- Does it name a format or a length? Move that to the second sentence.
- Does it contain a price? Move it.
- Could she repeat it to a friend from memory after reading it once? If not, it's too long.

Then put it in your shop bio, your link in bio, and the first line of your sales page. Same sentence in all three.
