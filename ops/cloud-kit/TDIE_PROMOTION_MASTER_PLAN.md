# THE DIGITAL INCOME EDIT™ · PROMOTION MASTER PLAN
### Single live copy, edited in place · Created 13 November 2026 (Decision 132) · Governs every scheduled task and session that writes, schedules or posts promotion

> **Where this sits.** Canon (`TDIE_CANON.md`, `canon.json`) wins over this file. Every name, price and link comes from `canon.json → products[]` and `key_pages` only. Nothing here invents a product, price, link or call to action. If a row below disagrees with the live shop, Skool or site, the live source wins and this row is stale.

> **Why this file exists.** On 13 November 2026 Jodie asked why 8 of about 75 items were being promoted. The answer was that no master list existed, the spread rules capped how often anything could run, and most items had no home on any platform. Jodie then said: "Remove my limits and rules regarding promotions." Decision 132 removed every limit. This file is the list that replaces them: every sellable item, where it runs, how often, and where it leads.

## 1 · THE ONE RULE

**Every item in `products[]` that is not held (Section 7) is promoted, on a rolling basis, on every platform that can carry it. There is no daily, weekly or repeat cap on any of it.** The grids below are the floor, not the ceiling. A loader or writer that has room adds more. Fresh words every time, because a repeated post is a weaker post, but that is craft and not a limit.

**What stays, because it is truth or platform mechanics and not a limit:**
- Instagram: no links anywhere, calls to action point to the link in bio. Facebook: link only in the first comment, 5 minutes after the post. Skool links buried in call-to-action words, never printed. (Decisions 115 and 117.)
- Pinterest blocks the whole domain, so pins go to Skool destinations only.
- Every membership link goes to `https://www.skool.com/thedigitalincomeedit/about`.
- Amazon and affiliate disclosure travels with every affiliate link (Decision 130, Standing Rule 18).
- Exact prices, full trademark names on first reference, no manufactured scarcity, no invented proof, real deadlines only, no refunds on digital products.
- Membership Standard is not sold inside the Skool feed (nearly all members already hold it free for life). Standard sells to cold traffic on every other platform.
- The 11:00 AM Skool slot teaches, the 8:00 AM slot is the Daily Free Prompt. Promotion lives in 3:00 PM and 7:00 PM, and everywhere else.
- Income and earnings claims are allowed (Decision 131), real numbers only.

## 2 · THE FUNNEL (plain English)

1. **Strangers find us** through Pinterest pins (to Skool pages), Threads, Instagram, the Facebook group and Amazon pins.
2. **They take something free.** Find Your Door quiz, The Weekend Build Challenge, The Faceless Income Blueprint™, the checklist, the cheat sheet, the Shopify Starter Kit, the One-Sentence Offer tool. Free is the door. Every free thing leads to an email list.
3. **Email turns them into buyers.** The challenge emails already sell Membership Standard and The Weekend Ecosystem™. The new 5-email follow-up (Section 8) sits behind the free kits.
4. **First purchase is small.** Value Vault guides at $2 to $19, The Operating Prompts™ at $19, The While-You-Sleep Storefront™ at $37.
5. **Then the big three.** The Weekend Ecosystem™ at $97, the Offer Edit, Funnel Edit and Scaling & Systems modules, and Membership.
6. **Members who want it done for them** buy a DFY service ($97 to $350). Members who want to earn buy the Weekend Ecosystem™ affiliate program (30%) or Storefront affiliate (40%).
7. **Alongside everything**, the affiliate lane (Amazon, Brand Closet, Earn With Skool, AIM and others) earns on the audience we already have.
