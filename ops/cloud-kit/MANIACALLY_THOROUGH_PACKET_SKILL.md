# MANIACALLY THOROUGH production packet

Brand: "True crime. Medical mysteries. Rabbit holes." Handle @maniacally.thorough.
Signature line: "I read the whole thing. Unfortunately for you." Payoff: "And I brought the receipts."
Philosophy: **Facts first. Rabbit holes second. Verify before you vilify.**

Jodie is a BSN-prepared RN and acute-care NP student. She films talking-head takes herself and uploads folders back. Do the sourcing, clipping and writing for her — she should only have to film.

## Non-negotiable: verification before production

- Never write a timestamp into a document that hasn't been checked against the source's own caption track. Cut clips from caption timings, then confirm the words.
- Tag every claim by confidence. Anything not verified verbatim goes in a quarantine section, never in the script.
- Quotes go on screen in quotation marks ONLY if verified word-for-word against a primary document.
- Where a figure rests on a partisan or secondary source, say so in bold and tell her to confirm it before filming.
- Give both halves of every damning statistic. If 75% were preventable, 12% were not — the packet must make it impossible to cut only the damning half.

## Where files live

On her PC under `Videos\`: `MT_V0n_CLIPS\{FINAL_CLIPS, SOURCE, TAKES, VISUALS}` plus `Videos\_MT_TOOLS` (yt-dlp, ffmpeg, pull scripts, cleaned transcripts).

Sandboxed shells can't reach YouTube or Wikimedia — downloads run natively in Windows via a `.bat` launched with computer use (File Explorer, click tier, double-click the file). Cut clips afterwards with ffmpeg over the mounted folder. Commit deliverables to her folders AND send them to the chat; files written to her PC never appear as chat cards.

## Required document sections

Every packet, in order:

1. **What this video argues** — the one question, and explicitly what it does NOT argue.
2. **Record these takes** — word-for-word scripts, one continuous clip each, named `V0n_TAKE_0n`, hook recorded twice.
3. **The receipts** — per clip: file path, source URL, verified timestamp, speaker, type (attorney argument / question / expert opinion / witness testimony), EXACT WORDS, **PROVES**, and **DOES NOT PROVE**.
4. **Assets / visuals** — sourced files with licence and required attribution; text cards where no safely-licensed image exists.
5. **Assembly order** — numbered, hard cuts, 9:16 1080×1920 canvas set before positioning.
6. **Overlay sheet** — cream on dark, lower third, 250px clear bottom / 120px top, nothing over a face, every overlay clears before the next cut.
7. **Audio** — voice and source audio only, no music, never speak over a witness.
8. **Posting package** — cover text, search phrase, caption, pinned comment, five hashtags (no filler).
9. **Fact-check table** — claim / receipt / status, including rows marked NOT ESTABLISHED — DO NOT SAY THIS.
10. **Rules that stay on** — per-video hard limits.
11. **QC checklist** — tick boxes before export.
12. **Status** — what's done, what's hers.
13. **Retention design** — where attention is won and lost, beat by beat. Explicitly not timecodes to hit.
14. **Search & discovery** — search phrase said aloud and on screen; natural language, no keyword stuffing; no #FYP or reach tags; Creator Search Insights shapes the next topic, never the evidence.
15. **TikTok safety & recommendation audit** — hard-no checklist: no engagement bait or spoken CTA, no AI imagery as evidence, no fabricated sources, no gore, nothing that contradicts the script.
16. **Currency check — posting day only** — facts that go stale, with source links and instructions to re-verify the morning she publishes.

Sections 13–16 are mandatory. They were added as a standing requirement.

## Standing editorial rules

- Never state guilt, innocence, motive, or mental state.
- Attorney argument, attorney question, attorney characterization, expert opinion and witness testimony are each labeled on screen as what they are. Never adopt an attorney's characterization as a finding.
- Never imply evidence was hidden, planted, or mishandled when a witness gave a normal professional reason.
- No photographs of minors or of private individuals in these cases. Say on camera why not — it's the most on-brand moment available.
- No logos of institutions — a logo reads as endorsement.
- Where she lacks the professional standing to adjudicate (forensic pathology, anaesthesia), she says so ON CAMERA, not just in the caption.
- Critique reasoning, never people. No remarks on a witness's manner or appearance.
- Source footage from public proceedings: short excerpts inside original commentary with attribution. Keep clips under ~15s. Fair-use posture, not a guarantee.
- Include the honesty beat — the caveat that weakens her own strongest point. It's what makes the rest credible, and it pre-empts the correcting commenter.

## Voice

Hooks are contradictions, not conclusions. "Neither did the police" (sourced) beats "I don't think she jumped" (her opinion) — a hook that announces the conclusion fights the channel's own posture. Never lock a runtime; the video runs as long as the verified material. Hard end, no outro, no end card.
