# Internal vs Customer Boundary

## Internal
Jodie's production machine may use:
- private browser sessions
- private account identifiers
- private logs
- exact board names
- exact task IDs
- internal command-centre state
- private Skool locations

## Customer
The While-You-Sleep Storefront™ receives:
- templates
- setup interview
- generic recipes
- buyer-owned log structure
- buyer-owned browser lock
- buyer-owned schedules
- buyer-owned links
- buyer-owned boards
- generic worked examples

## Translation rule

When an internal process becomes customer-facing:

**Do not copy it. Abstract it.**

Example:

Internal:
`trig_017tQcAgu8D9LxjG3MEmNXx3 runs Sun-Fri at 9:20 pm ET`

Customer:
`Create a nightly task on the days you want new looks prepared. Choose a time when your computer is normally on and your browser session is available.`

Internal:
`Pink Outfit of the Day | Amazon Fashion Finds`

Customer:
`YOUR PUBLIC OUTFIT BOARD`

Internal:
`claude/BRAND_CLOSET_PIN_LOG.md`

Customer:
`storefront-log.md`

That rule applies to every private path, identifier and account-specific instruction.
