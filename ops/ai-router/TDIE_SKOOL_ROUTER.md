# TDIE Skool router V2

## Non-negotiable separation
Skool has at least four independent workstreams:
1. Daily Prompts lesson + Daily Free Prompt community post (8:00 AM Eastern). Governed ONLY by the Daily Prompts router.
2. Premium DFY Content Calendar (Premium membership content). Governed ONLY by the Premium calendar router.
3. General community feed, member engagement and weekly scheduled posts. Governed by current approved Skool posting system.
4. Product launch, course access, pricing and all-member email. Founder approval required for any live commercial change.

The paid DFY Viral Instagram Content Calendar is a separate product. Do not route it through the Premium member calendar or Daily Prompts.

## ChatGPT
Create community copy and scheduling packets; review campaign strategy; QA audience, tier and course-gate intent; never assume a live post was published.

## Claude
Read the live Skool account; load approved posts/lessons; verify actual course records and scheduled items by reading back from the platform. For course access changes, a successful save is NOT proof; read the actual course record back after every write. Never remove grandfathered Standard access.

## Scheduling safeguards
- Daily Prompts and Premium Calendar never share assets, queues, task instructions or post IDs.
- Avoid duplicate Skool feed posts from simultaneous scheduled loaders.
- Check the platform's current all-member-email cooldown before scheduling launch blasts; do not infer from an old 72-hour example.
- Use exact approved membership pricing and tier access from the live account, not a September snapshot.
- All course price, gate and tier changes require Jodie's approval.
- For Storefront launch lessons, read the live-test release gate before publishing.

## Reporting
Read-only inventory first. Every actual write must have read-back evidence; mark missing evidence UNVERIFIED, never DONE.
