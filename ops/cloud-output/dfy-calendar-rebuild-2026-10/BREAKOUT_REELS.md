## The strongest evidence-backed lesson

The breakout is not necessarily the first "good" Reel.

In the deep creator datasets, large posts often sit on top of an existing sequence of:
- repeated topic testing
- repeated hook testing
- repeated format testing
- growing audience familiarity

The breakout Reel is the post where the existing ingredients combine with an unusually strong topic/packaging fit.

## Duration

The largest current quantitative Reel dataset reports a U-shaped relationship:
- 7-12 seconds: 11.7% viral
- 20-45 seconds: 8.6-9.0%
- 60-90 seconds: 10.9%

Within accounts, 12-second-or-shorter Reels beat longer Reels 59% of the time.

This is a corpus-level relationship, not evidence that every breakout should be short or long.
