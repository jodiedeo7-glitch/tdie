# OCTOBER 2026 DFY CALENDAR REPLICATION RULES

Status: PROVISIONAL. These rules are NOT authorized for calendar creation until the 40 qualifying-outlier / 25-account research gate passes.

R01 - Lead with the problem, not the generic topic.
Evidence: R01-R08, R10-R18, R22-R30.
Use: Open with the exact frustration, mistake, question, or decision the intended reader is already carrying.
Do not copy competitor wording.

R02 - Make the first promise concrete.
Evidence: R13, R17, R23, R26, R28, R30, R34, R39, R40.
Use: Prefer a number, setting, checklist, tool, step, example, or specific task over broad "tips" language.

R03 - Use list and framework structures when the content genuinely contains a list.
Evidence: R13, R23, R25, R26, R28, R30, R31, R34, R37, R39, R40.
Use: 3, 5, 6 or similar bounded structures when each item earns its place.
Do not force listicles onto ideas that are better taught through story or demonstration.

R04 - Pair personal context with practical payoff.
Evidence: R01, R06-R08, R11, R19, R21, R38.
Use: When using story, move quickly from "what happened" to "what I would do differently" or another concrete takeaway.
Do not create invented personal stories.

R05 - Demonstrate the mechanism.
Evidence: R12, R17, R22, R23, R25-R27, R30, R38, R40.
Use: Show the setting, steps, example, screen, workflow, or decision rather than merely asserting that something works.
Do not fabricate screenshots, results, metrics, or proof.

R06 - Make beginner relevance explicit.
Evidence: R05, R10, R12, R15-R18, R20, R29, R38, R40.
Use: Define what the beginner can do next without assuming advanced skills.

R07 - Resource CTAs can be a strong conversion bridge.
Evidence: R09, R12, R16, R28, R31-R33, R39.
Use: A small next step such as a checklist or resource can bridge an educational post to the next action.
TDIE-specific limit: do not use a comment-to-DM or link-in-comment mechanic that conflicts with canon.

R08 - Contrarian framing should create tension, not false certainty.
Evidence: R01, R24-R25, R27-R28.
Use: "Stop doing X" or "Before you post X" only when the claim can be defended.
Do not use exaggerated guarantees or invented algorithm claims.

R09 - Tool content works best when tied to a job.
Evidence: R17, R28, R30-R37, R39.
Use: Organize AI/tool content around a beginner task or outcome rather than a generic dump of tools.

R10 - Person-free production is a legitimate creative option.
Evidence: limited. R08 and R21 mention face-free or B-roll workflows, but most available sources did not expose image frames directly.
Status: LOW CONFIDENCE. Do not turn this into a strong rule until direct visual inspection supplies more evidence.

NON-REPLICABLE PATTERNS:
- earnings/income claims on Meta
- faith-first content
- raw-link or prohibited link mechanics
- comment-to-DM mechanisms where they conflict with TDIE's no-DM rule
- fabricated urgency, proof, follower counts, or revenue
