Important evidence limitations:
- Opening-frame visuals were not directly inspectable for most entries in the accessible search results, so visual pattern counts are NOT VERIFIED.
- Exact posting times were not reliably available and were not used as a research rule.
- Raw comment CTA frequency is an observed competitor tactic. TDIE cannot automatically reproduce competitor DM/link mechanics because current canon has stricter Instagram link and DM rules.
- Some entries are adjacent niches or larger accounts. Those were retained as pattern candidates but not treated as clean proof of account-relative outlier behavior.

Preliminary pattern interpretation:
1. Specificity beats vague inspiration in the observed sample. The strongest examples name a problem, number, setting, tool, or concrete task quickly.
2. Lists and short frameworks are common vehicles for practical utility.
3. Personal experience becomes more useful when it resolves a specific objection or teaches a repeatable action.
4. Tool content performs as a bridge between curiosity and action, especially when it answers a narrow beginner task.
5. Contrarian openings create a clear tension device, but TDIE should keep the claim factual and avoid manufactured certainty.
