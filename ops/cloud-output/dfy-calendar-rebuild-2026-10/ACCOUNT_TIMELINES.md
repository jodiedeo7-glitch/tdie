## @sam.gaudet

Source: Content Copilot playbook. 179 Reels analyzed from September 2025 through May 2026.

Pre-breakout context:
- Jan 10: 383.5K, AI tools for businesses, list
- Jan 11: 317.6K, contrarian brand statement
- Jan 14: 267.8K, specific-result/trial-Reels explanation
- Feb 19: 222.1K, AI-tool list
- Feb 20: 428.5K, contrarian ChatGPT claim
- Feb 21: 163.5K, contrarian ChatGPT claim
- Mar 2: 188.4K, story/question
- Mar 10: 40.4K, AI-tool stack with comment CTA

Breakout Reel:
Mar 14, 2026. "If I wanted to go from $0 to $1M using AI, I would do these 5 things." 1.9M views. Content Copilot reports an 18.4K account median, making this approximately 103X median.

Breakout characteristics:
- numbered list
- AI topic
- aspirational outcome
- specific promise
- talking-head format
- no major format change from normal account behavior

Afterward:
- Mar 25: 158.6K
- Mar 29: 160.3K
- Mar 31: 156.7K

Diagnosis:
The 1.9M Reel is the strongest observed breakout in the period, but not the first indication of strong account-level performance. The account had already tested AI topics, list structures and contrarian hooks. The breakout appears to be a stronger combination of topic-market fit + packaging + aspirational framing rather than a sudden switch to a new production format.

What was normal:
Talking head dominated 91.1% of the account's Reels. Viral-goal content was 79.9%.

What was different:
The 1.9M Reel stacked a highly salient AI/business topic with a numbered hypothetical and a concrete aspirational promise.

Causation:
NOT PROVEN.

Repeatability:
The account continued producing large posts after the breakout, supporting a repeatable topic/hook system rather than a one-off format change.

## @personalbrandlaunch

Source: Content Copilot playbook. 1,423 Reels analyzed from March 2023 through April 2026.

Breakout cluster:
- Jul 3, 2024: 1.6M, Reel transformation/result-first
- Jul 10, 2024: 5.0M, ChatGPT prompt
- Aug 5, 2024: 7.4M, hypothetical Instagram strategy
- Oct 1, 2024: 3.9M, prompt improvement
- Nov 6, 2024: 1.3M, warning format

Account context:
The source states that the largest 2024 hits occurred while the account was scaling from roughly 400K to 600K followers.

Core pattern:
The account did not invent a new formula for each breakout. It repeatedly reused 15-20 content formulas, including:
- "If I had to start from scratch" 10+ uses
- "100 views vs 1M views" 15+ uses
- recurring personal-story hooks 8+ uses

Diagnosis:
This is best classified as FORMULA BREAKOUT / PACKAGING BREAKOUT. The data supports repeated refinement of proven structures.

Causation:
NOT PROVEN. The source itself notes topic-market fit and the account's growing warm audience likely affect the absolute ceiling.
