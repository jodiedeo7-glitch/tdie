## Maintenance-cycle finding

The research does NOT support a fixed "Week 1 introduction -> Week 2 education -> Week 3 nurture -> Week 4 sales" calendar.

The larger creator datasets show reach/education, authority, story/community and conversion content operating as parallel functions rather than four permanent monthly phases. @personalbrandlaunch, for example, has 87.1% viral-goal content, 7.9% client-acquisition content, 3.8% community/story and 1.1% brand/manifesto across a 1,423-reel corpus, demonstrating a mixed content ecosystem rather than a monthly onboarding cycle. @sam.gaudet similarly separates reach, brand and community/client functions while targeting an already-established audience.

For TDIE, the correct interpretation is a rolling maintenance cycle:
- remind people who you are when useful
- continually attract new people
- continually teach
- continually build authority
- continually create connection
- continually engage
- continually handle objections
- continually sell, subject to offer spacing rules

No month should behave as though the account has been reset to zero.
