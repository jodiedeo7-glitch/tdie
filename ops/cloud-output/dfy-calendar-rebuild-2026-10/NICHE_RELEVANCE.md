# OCTOBER 2026 DFY CALENDAR - NICHE RELEVANCE PASS

Research date: 29 September 2026

## Conclusion

Long-form is relevant to TDIE selectively, but should remain an occasional HeyGen talking-head exception.

Eligible categories:
1. talking-head education
2. talking-head strategy / hypothetical
3. talking-head case study / transformation
4. talking-head founder story / lesson
5. talking-head objection handling / authority explanation

Do not add long cinematic or faceless generated Reels to the normal DFY workflow.

## Long-form classification

### A — eligible for occasional HeyGen long-form
- "If I had to start my digital-product business from zero today..."
- "Here's exactly how I'd build my first $1,000/month digital-product funnel..."
- evidence-backed founder/client case study
- substantive objection handling
- a strategy that cannot be honestly delivered in a very short Reel

### B — relevant idea, but compress to short-form
- result + timeframe stories
- faceless digital-marketing transformations
- short lists
- AI tool recommendations
- Instagram growth tips
- simple funnel tips
- quick product-creation tips
- freebie/keyword CTA concepts

### C — do not import
- generic lifestyle DITL status content
- creator-specific status flexes
- long cinematic montage
- long faceless aesthetic mood videos
- long tutorials that can be delivered quickly
- any long Reel selected solely because the source happened to be long

## October rule

Use approximately two HeyGen-compatible long-form talking-head concepts at most.

Everything else remains short-form.

Long-form research evidence is not automatically a long-form production recommendation. Source duration is not copied into the DFY calendar.
