#### 11:00 AM · TEACH
Etsy is a marketplace shelf. Your store is a store. Four differences that cost people money.

1/ The shelf owns the customer. You get an order, they get the email address. Every sale on your own store builds a list. Every sale on the shelf builds theirs.

2/ The shelf sets the rules. Fees, search, suspensions. One policy change and your income moves. On your store the only policy is yours.

3/ The shelf is crowded on purpose. Your listing sits beside nine copies of it. Your store has one product on the page: yours.

4/ Sample it before you sell it. Nothing is made until it's paid for. Same rule, both places, but only one of them lets you keep the buyer.

How to write your first five emails before you have a single subscriber.

1/ Email one: what you built and why. Not a welcome. A story with a reason.

2/ Email two: the mistake you made so she doesn't. Specific. The cost in hours or dollars.

3/ Email three: the thing you'd do this week if you were her. One step. Not five.

4/ Email four: the objection. The reason she hasn't done it yet, said out loud, and what you'd say back.

5/ Email five: the offer, as a fact. What it is, what it costs, where it is. No "I don't usually do this".

Write them now. Subscriber one gets the same five as subscriber one thousand.
