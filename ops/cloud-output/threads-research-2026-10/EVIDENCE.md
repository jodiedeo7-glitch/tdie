# EVIDENCE: post-by-post notes, Threads viral research (Job 13, Part 1)

Written 27 September 2026. Companion to `REPORT.md` and `ACCOUNTS.csv` in this folder.

## How this evidence was gathered, and what it is not

- The cloud session's network policy blocked every page fetch to threads.com, instagram.com, tiktok.com, youtube.com, medium.com, substack.com, buffer.com, blacktwist.app, dev.to, reddit.com, gumroad.com, skool.com and the podcast hosts. Nothing below comes from an opened page.
- Everything comes from web search results. Threads exposes a post's text as the page title of the post URL, so search results return post text verbatim for indexed posts. Profile results return the display name, bio and a follower count as indexed (date of the crawl unknown).
- Labels: **VERIFIED** = the text was seen in a search excerpt of that exact Threads, Instagram or other page, with the link. **REPORTED** = the creator or a third party says so on a linked page. **INFERRED** = my reasoning, always with the reason. Post dates written "approx" are inferred from the post ID ordering (Threads IDs run in time order; anchors: ID DJVaJ38NkC0 = 6 May 2025, DRtzlKtDHHd = 1 Dec 2025, DTRuTKrEk1o = 8 Jan 2026, DVPysdQE3PN = 26 Feb 2026) and may be off by a month.
- Not visible for any account: per-post likes, replies, reposts or views (unless the creator stated them), profile pictures, pinned posts, posting times, whether a post carried an image, reply threads, and account creation dates.
- Nine parallel search agents plus my own searches ran about 230 queries. A shared search cap cut every agent short of its target, so coverage is uneven and the gaps are named at the end.
- Em dashes inside quoted posts were replaced with commas or hyphens. Spelling inside quotes is the original.

## Dataset and platform evidence (post-level, from excerpts)

BlackTwist monthly reports (its own users; floors of 100 followers and 1,000 views; not a random sample):
- Jan 2026: 291 creators, 26,103 posts. 500+ replies received in the month: 22.5% average growth vs 15.1% for under 10. 10+ posts a week: 8.3% median growth vs 3.0% for 1 to 2 a week (the same excerpt gives 22.65% for 5 to 6 a week, which does not fit; needs the page). Long-form threads 14,343 average views. https://blacktwist.app/report/january-2026
- Feb 2026: 255 creators, 20,717 posts. Long-form threads 5,072 average views. 100+ replies: median growth near 10%. Daily and twice-daily posters strongest median growth; 8 of the top 10 by growth rate posted 10+ times a week. Saturday best reach. https://blacktwist.app/report/february-2026
- Mar 2026: 21,864 posts, 283 ranked creators (1,867 accounts with a post). "The single strongest growth signal tracked is replies": 500+ replies gives 10.09% median growth. 5 to 6 posts a week best median growth at 8.87%; 7 to 10 a week averaged 23.47% but median 3.35% (outliers). Short posts best engagement rate 6.09%; long-form threads most views 4,416. 8 of the top 10 fastest growers started under 10,000 followers, three under 1,500. Saturday best reach, Friday best engagement. Conclusion on the page: "the algorithm cares about conversation more than anything else." https://blacktwist.app/blog/we-analyzed-21-864-threads-posts-in-march-2026-here-s-what-we-found
- Jul 2026: 464 accounts, 139,350 posts, 354M views (2.7x June). Reply ladder: median growth 2.90% (under 10 replies) rising at every rung to 19.00% (500+). 10+ posts a week: average 43.80%, median 7.77%. Long-form threads 12,899 average views, "nearly 18x a short post"; short posts best engagement rate 7.84%. A summary labelled August but carrying July's sample: text-only posts 6,259 average views vs 2,501 with media; best reach at 100 to 199 characters (6,403). Sunday best reach, Monday best engagement. Top growers "thread-first and relentless" (@climbtowealth 1,210 posts in the month; @pooravbolar 1,244). Named growers: @rosettewilson_ 3,750 to 53,503; @julietemirella 93,196 to 199,667 (this last figure supports the 201.3K profile excerpt for A02). https://blacktwist.app/report/july-2026
- Timing study, 21 Feb to 23 Mar 2026, 109,300 posts, 1,117 creators: "late morning to early afternoon dominates during the week" in local time. https://blacktwist.app/blog/best-times-to-post-on-threads
- Apr, May, Jun 2026 pages exist (319, 341, 365 creators) but no findings surfaced. No 2025 reports exist (series began Jan 2026, INFERRED).
