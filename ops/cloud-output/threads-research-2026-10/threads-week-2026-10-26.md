Print on demand vs dropshipping. Which to start with, decided in four lines.

1/ Print on demand: you design, they print and ship. Lower risk, thinner margin, your brand on the product. Start here if you have a look and no stock.

2/ Dropshipping: you list, they ship. Wider choice, thinner brand, more supplier risk. Start here if you have a niche and no design.

3/ Both: nothing is made until it's paid for. Both: sample it before you sell it. Both: the store is yours, so the customer is too.

4/ Neither is the business. The offer and the list are. Pick one method this week and stop reading comparisons. Including mine.
