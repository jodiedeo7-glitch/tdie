## Recommendation: (a) Shopify Basic plus the CJ Dropshipping app, with Zendrop as the paid fallback

Shopify Basic with the free CJ app is the only stack where the whole order flow, including tax collection, runs without a person, and it costs $39 a month (three dollars total for the first three months on the intro offer). The Starter plan is closed to new stores, so option (b) does not exist in 2026, and option (c) saves the $39 only by turning every order into a manual session on Jodie's PC, which is the one thing the business does not have spare. Start on CJ because it is free and has US warehouses on both coasts; if the desktop check finds fewer than 30 of the 40 bank products in CJ US stock, add Zendrop at $29/month rather than compromise on warehouse country.

Two conditions: the store runs on a **new domain** (Pinterest's block is on thedigitalincomeedit.com; the two domains never link to each other from Pinterest), and the Shopify link she uses to open the store is her own affiliate link `https://shopify.pxf.io/eK3xYz` (canon Decision 83), because it pays her for her own signup.

## What Jodie must do herself before the first sale (facts with sources, not legal advice)

Full list with steps and links in `LEGAL_SETUP_CHECKLIST.md`. The short version:

1. **Georgia sales tax registration** at the Georgia Tax Center (free; account number emailed in about 15 minutes) and **economic nexus in other states** ($100,000 of sales in a state in 41 of 46 jurisdictions; 17 states have dropped the 200-transaction test as of 1 Aug 2026). Unverified: the "free" and "4%" figures come from third-party pages, not a dor.georgia.gov snippet.
2. **Refund and return policy page.** No federal mandate; California, New York and Florida each require the policy to be posted (or a statutory refund right applies). Pinterest's merchant guidelines require a clear, easy-to-find returns policy with contact details.
3. **Privacy policy and terms page.** CCPA/CPRA applies above $25 million revenue or 100,000 consumers, so it will not apply on day one, but Pinterest and Shopify Payments expect the pages, and Shopify's admin generates templates.
4. **Product liability by category.** Textiles (the pajama set, robe, throw, seat belt covers) fall under the Flammable Fabrics Act and 16 CFR 1610; candles and anything with a battery or plug are kept out of the bank on purpose; the tumbler and cups are food-contact items. Ask each supplier for the test report before listing. General liability insurance cost: not researched (unverified).
5. **FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule** (16 CFR 435): you must have a reasonable basis for any stated ship time (30 days if none is stated), and on a delay you must get the customer's consent or refund without being asked. This is why every product page in D1 states a real window and why the order-by dates exist.
6. **Pinterest merchant guidelines** for the new store domain: claim the domain, post shipping and returns policies, show contact details, no counterfeit goods, then the Verified Merchant Program once the site has orders.
7. **De minimis is gone** (all countries, 29 August 2025; China and Hong Kong from 2 May 2025), which is why the bank is US-warehouse first and why AliExpress Choice is a last resort only.
8. **Business registration**: Georgia LLC articles of organization are $100 online at ecorp.sos.ga.gov ($110 by mail), annual registration $60; a sole proprietor needs no state filing; a trade name is filed with the county Superior Court clerk (Fortson sits in Muscogee and Harris counties). Confirm which entity she wants with her accountant.
