### 11:00 · TEACH

Write the manual steps before you automate the task.

Include the check after the work, not just the instruction to do it.

A tool saying "done" and the result being right are separate things.

SELF-REPLY 1

1. List the trigger, the inputs and the action. Include where the finished work is supposed to live.

SELF-REPLY 3

3. Define the evidence you'll keep. Reopen the saved result or read it back from the destination before marking it complete.
