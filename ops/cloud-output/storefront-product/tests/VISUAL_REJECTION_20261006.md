# Client visual rejection - 6 October 2026

## Live result and execution defects

The customer rejected the finished pink-desk test Pin as visually unacceptable. Its technical generation, dimension, link and readability checks had been recorded separately, but the operator incorrectly treated those as sufficient visual acceptance. The raw images showed a generic front-facing tabletop product lineup rather than the selected flat-lay/collage treatment. "Clean" was interpreted as empty objects and no scene styling. Corrections changed organizer proportions while retaining weak art direction. The final oversized headline and generic boxed CTA compounded the problem.

The two-product packet came from an earlier Amazon capability test. It was then incorrectly presented as a first-look scheduling candidate without the complete curated look required by the recipe. No Pin was approved or scheduled. The customer's rejection supersedes the operator's visual PASS and withdraws the pending scheduling request.

## Source repairs and proposed replacement

- Apply the visual standard to every product/theme/roundup image, not only outfit inspiration.
- Give clean/BASIC images the same photographic craft and desirability gates as editorial/EXPANDED images.
- Require a specific arrangement, surface, viewpoint, light, material treatment and justified scene styling before generation. Separate unlinked styling props from extra featured products.
- Check actual flat-lay/collage format and the visual result independently from job completion, readability and working destinations.
- Choose typography from the photograph's hierarchy; bold is not a fixed oversized headline template.
- Keep capability samples separate from the complete first-look test. Retire a rejected approval request and fix the overall brief before additional detail corrections.
