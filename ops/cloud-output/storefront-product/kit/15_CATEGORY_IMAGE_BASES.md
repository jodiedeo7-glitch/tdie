## Assemble one complete prompt

Use one category and one concept. Resolve every field from the confirmed client curation profile and verified product packet. Do not send placeholders, alternative camera instructions, sourcing notes, affiliate links or client-flow explanations to the image model.

1. Opening: "Original photorealistic editorial product photography, portrait 2:3, with {{AESTHETIC}} art direction and a coordinated {{PALETTE}} palette. Photograph {{PRODUCTS}} for {{USE}}."
2. Add the selected category's CLEAN composition paragraph below, or replace it with its approved STYLED or LIFESTYLE composition. Never append a conflicting camera instruction. The assistant expands the shorter variation using that category's actual geometry and product facts.
3. Add the client finish: "Use {{SURFACE}} with {{LIGHT}}. Preserve the products' verified colors, material, construction and relative scale. Use only these incidental unlinked styling props: {{APPROVED_PROPS}}. Reserve {{TEXT_SPACE}} as quiet photo space, with the products still leading the composition. Realistic material texture, coherent contact shadows and clean photographic craft. No text, logos, brand names, prices, ratings, watermark, extra featured products or cropped product edges."
4. For a persona lifestyle scene, use the required reference-only opening and approved PERSONA WORLD from 07_PERSONA_PATHS.txt. Without an authorized identity reference, use an approved person-free in-situ concept. Never invent a client persona.

{{PRODUCTS}} is a plain-language list of visible verified attributes, not names/ASINs/URLs. {{AESTHETIC}}, {{PALETTE}}, {{SURFACE}}, {{LIGHT}}, {{APPROVED_PROPS}} and {{ENVIRONMENT}} come from confirmed client answers. {{USE}} is one concrete purpose. {{SEASON}} applies only when selected. Default text-safe space is a modest quiet area, roughly 10-15 percent of the composition, adjusted to the actual headline. Do not create a huge empty wall to accommodate giant type. Retail colors and product geometry are fixed, not client-aesthetic variables.

## Stable structure and controlled variation

Keep the category's camera, physical arrangement logic and fidelity rules stable. Change only the client's style layer, verified product substitutions, use/season, restrained styling and the selected sibling concept. Save BASE_ID, BASE_VERSION, CONCEPT, resolved inputs, full assembled prompt, model/settings, returned job/image and observed QA. Change one major variable per comparison and preserve failures. Use the same recipe for both automatic and manual/free runs; the execution route does not downgrade quality.

A board is a destination, not always an image category. Cute finds is an umbrella: route to the actual product category. Gifts and seasonal/holiday inherit their objects' physical-category geometry. A mixed look gets one primary category with a deliberate hierarchy, not a concatenation of several incompatible bases. OTHER requires a short new geometry-specific base before generation.

Clean means considered, product-first and fewer distractions. It does not mean empty, bare or generic. Styled means a distinct arrangement/material story, not clutter. Lifestyle means an actual use moment, not the same product shot with a different headline. A flat lay is directly overhead; a dimensional product scene is labeled accurately.
