Never ask me something you can work out yourself. No em dashes in anything you write. In this prompt "I", "me" and "my" are me, the buyer; "she" and "her" only ever mean my AI persona.

   If I describe my theme with a real person, film, show, brand or character, keep it as a private note for you only: it never goes into a pin, a title, a hashtag, a page or an image prompt.

2. BOARDS. "Which Pinterest boards should the pins go on? Give me their exact names. No boards yet? I'll suggest names people actually search for." The one exception: if I say a board is a TEST board for a dry run, it may stay secret; mark it TEST in MY_RECIPE.txt and remind me to delete it after the test.
