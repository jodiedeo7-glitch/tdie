AUTOMATION 1: THE THEMED-LOOK RECIPE
The weekly task reads this file and MY_RECIPE.txt at the start of every run. You never edit this file. Your theme, boards, times and path live in MY_RECIPE.txt.

WHAT ONE LOOK PRODUCES
One look = 5 to 8 Amazon pieces that go together (a roundup: 3 or 5), one list or page that links every piece, and two pins that point to it, posted 3 days apart.

Three kinds of look, all built the same way:
  Outfit: one full outfit. Main piece (dress, set, or top plus bottom), shoes, bag, at least one piece of jewelry, one accessory, and optionally one beauty item or one book (a plain clothbound book with no title showing, named by its kind, never its title or author).
  Theme list: 5 to 8 matching finds for one space or purpose (a room, a desk, a car, a party table, a gift guide).
  Roundup: 3 or 5 options of one item ("3 cozy cardigans for...").

====================================================================
PART 1. THE RULES THAT NEVER MOVE
====================================================================

R1. Publishing is handled by the selected publishing path in MY_RECIPE.txt. The recipe does not use Pinterest scraping, Pinterest internal endpoints, or browser automation to simulate Pinterest publishing.
R2. The TIME-SAVING path uses the customer's connected Metricool account when the current ChatGPT + Metricool workflow is available and has passed the customer's test publish. The CREDIT-SAVING path prepares the complete Pin package for the customer to schedule through Pinterest's native scheduler.
R3. Every pin description starts with "#ad" and ends with the DISCLOSURE LINE from MY_RECIPE.txt. A pin with a link and no disclosure, or a disclosure and no link, does not ship.
R4. No prices, anywhere. Not on the image, title, description, alt text, site page, look idea, or item name.
R5. No third-party IP in any image. No film stills, character likenesses, logos, designer monograms, team or university crests, printed brand names, book titles, or other protected identifiers.
R6. No brand names in listings, Pin titles, descriptions, hashtags, alt text or page item names. Describe the item plainly. No real people, films, shows or characters in customer-facing content.
R7. The two Pins of one look are normally scheduled 3 days apart. Any near-identical Pins should be separated according to the schedule in MY_RECIPE.txt.
R8. No affiliate links in email. If you ever email about a look, link to your own site page, never directly to Amazon.
R9. Do not use Amazon-hosted product images as published assets. They are reference material only. Use original generated imagery or customer-owned/licensed imagery.
R10. At most 4 image-generation attempts per final asset: initial generation, up to two in-place corrections, and one fresh retry with the correction incorporated. If still wrong, drop the look and log why.
R11. Verify every required destination before a Pin enters the publishing queue. For the Influencer path, the Idea List must resolve. For Associates-only, the site page must resolve before publishing.
R12. Never schedule more than 14 days ahead in the storefront system, even though Pinterest and Metricool may support longer horizons. The 14-day limit is a product-state safety rule.
R13. If Pinterest exposes an AI-generated/AI-modified control in the selected publishing path, enable it when appropriate to the actual asset.
R14. Amazon affiliate links must come from the customer's authorized Amazon workflow. Never invent, alter or substitute a customer's affiliate tag.
R15. No em dashes in customer-generated copy.
R16. State is authoritative. Stable LOOK_ID and PIN_ID values prevent duplicate work. Human-readable markdown exports are views of state, not the source of truth.
R17. Do not silently switch image models, providers, or paid plans when a credit or access limit is reached. Record the waiting state and tell the customer what is required.
R18. Amazon product research must respect Amazon's current terms and the customer's authorized access. Do not scrape Amazon in the background or bypass access controls.

PART 2. PICK THE LOOKS
====================================================================

1. Read storefront-log.md and pin-drafts.md first. Leftovers (Part 7 step 4) go before anything new.
2. Look dates: the LOOK DAYS in MY_RECIPE.txt, starting with the first look day after today that has no look in storefront-log.md (any status except "dropped"). At most 4 looks per run.
3. Take each look from the THEME CALENDAR window that covers that look's own date. At least one look every week comes from the EVERGREEN BANK.
4. Mix. If MY_RECIPE.txt says both clothing and home, follow its MIX. Two outfit looks never land on back-to-back days: put a theme list or roundup between them. Outfit of the Day pins don't count toward this.
5. No repeats. The hero piece (the dress, set, top or statement item the look is built on) never repeats within 30 days, counting both automations' rows in storefront-log.md. Shoes, bags, jewelry and accessories may repeat, at most once a week each.
6. If a window has no unused ideas left, fill from the EVERGREEN BANK and tell me in one line to renew the calendar early.

PART 3. AMAZON PRODUCT INTAKE
====================================================================

The customer, not the automation, controls Amazon product selection unless the customer has a current authorized Amazon Creators API integration.

DEFAULT PATH: CUSTOMER-SUPPLIED PRODUCT ROWS

1. The customer adds product rows to PRODUCT_SOURCES.md or the structured state.

Required fields:
- ASIN
- plain-language product description
- authorized affiliate/Special Link when required by the selected Amazon path
- optional Amazon product URL
- optional color/variant
- optional notes

2. ChatGPT validates the supplied ASIN/link format and checks that the row has enough information to build the look.

3. ChatGPT does NOT:
- scrape Amazon pages
- simulate human browsing to collect product data
- capture affiliate links through browser automation
- copy Amazon-hosted product images into the image workflow
- invent ASINs
- invent affiliate tags
- use Amazon customer reviews or ratings as if they were independently sourced data

4. If a supplied row is incomplete:
Set PRODUCT_STATUS = intake_needed.
Tell the customer exactly which field is missing.
Do not silently substitute a different Amazon product.

ADVANCED PATH: AMAZON CREATORS API

If the customer has current, authorized Creators API access, use only the documented API fields and permissions available to that customer.

Record:
- API access = yes
- integration method
- retrieved product fields
- retrieval timestamp

Do not assume Creators API eligibility.
Do not build customer instructions around PA-API 5.

PRODUCT SHEET

Before image generation, create a product sheet from the customer's authorized product data:
- short name
- ASIN
- plain-language description
- color
- shape/cut
- material
- distinctive details
- authorized affiliate link or destination
- source method

Amazon-hosted images remain reference material only and are not published as Pin assets.

INFLUENCER PATH

Use the customer's approved Amazon Influencer tools to create the Idea List from the supplied ASINs or the customer's authorized product-selection workflow.

ASSOCIATES-ONLY PATH

Use the customer's authorized Special Links in the customer's own site/page workflow.

If an affiliate link or destination cannot be verified, the look remains queued.

PART 4. MAKE THE IMAGES
====================================================================

IMAGE EXECUTION

Read 13_PIN_VISUAL_STANDARD.md before generating an outfit inspiration Pin and apply its composition, headline and acceptance checks. A generated outfit is inspiration unless it depicts the actual linked items or uses clearly identified customer-owned/licensed product photos; match the Pin's title, destination, board, disclosure and alt text to that status.

Use the connected image-generation provider selected in MY_RECIPE.txt. The preferred current path is ChatGPT + Higgsfield when the customer has that integration connected.

Do not require a desktop browser, manual copy/paste, screenshots or a separate image-generation session when the connected provider can return the finished asset directly.

For each final asset:
1. Build the prompt from the product sheet, persona settings and look requirements.
2. Generate the asset once.
3. Inspect the result against the acceptance criteria.
4. If a correction is needed, use an in-place edit when the selected provider/model supports it.
5. Regenerate only when an edit cannot produce the required correction.
6. Record the generation job/asset identifier, provider and model in structured state.
7. Preserve the approved asset as the canonical IMAGE_ID and reuse it for every downstream Pin that uses that asset.

Do not generate a second image merely to add deterministic text or layout. Use deterministic rendering when that produces the same required final asset with less generation cost and no loss of quality.

Never make the customer copy an image from one application into another as the normal path.

PIN 1: THE FLAT LAY OR COLLAGE (no person)
Use the image provider and model configured in MY_RECIPE.txt and confirmed available for this run. Do not hard-code or silently substitute an image provider/model. If the configured model is unavailable, record the blocked/waiting state and report the required action.

Read the last look's Pin 1 composition in storefront-log.md and use the other composition this time. Favor a calm editorial composition with clear product hierarchy, restrained props, and comfortable background margins. Every garment and accessory must fit completely inside the 2:3 canvas; do not crop straps, hems, sleeves, shoes, or trouser legs. Leave a moderate clean area at the top for a headline.

Use the product sheet as text input in the prompt. Do not ask the image model to open or attach a text document. For each item, use customer-authorized product facts and describe only visible, supported attributes such as color, shape, material, and details. Use customer-owned or licensed image references only when the selected provider supports them and the customer has rights to use them. Never use Amazon-hosted product images as published assets or as image-generation references.

IMAGE PROMPT (fill brackets; do not request text):
Create a polished vertical 2:3 Pinterest [outfit inspiration / themed collection / product roundup] image. Arrange [the selected items, described individually using approved product facts] neatly on [a suitable restrained background]. Keep every item fully inside the canvas with comfortable margins on all sides. Leave a moderate uncluttered band across the top for a headline to be added after image generation. Use [visual style from MY_RECIPE.txt], soft natural light, realistic material detail, and balanced editorial spacing. Use only [zero to two relevant props, if any]. No text, letters, logos, labels, watermarks, brand marks, invented product details, or extra products. Do not crop, distort, or obscure any listed item.

TEXT AND LAYOUT:
Add the approved headline after image generation using the configured deterministic layout/template when available. Use a short search phrase in a high-contrast serif and an optional brief supporting phrase in restrained script. Keep all text inside the reserved top area and check spelling, contrast, and readability at phone size. If deterministic layout is unavailable, return the image without a headline for customer review; do not ask the image model to render exact typography.

PIN 2 depends on MY_RECIPE.txt and 07_PERSONA_PATHS.txt:
  PERSONA PATH, OUTFIT LOOKS: use the approved persona reference and the configured available image provider/model. Keep each item visible and consistent with customer-authorized product facts; otherwise make no unsupported exact-product claim.
  PERSONA PATH, THEME LISTS AND ROUNDUPS: use the alternate composition with the configured provider/model. Do not show a person with home items.
  NO-PERSONA PATH: use the alternate flat-lay composition with a different surface/background and a distinct approved headline.

Refine, never restart: if something's off (wrong color, a missing piece, something cut off at the edge), say exactly what to change in the same chat. Two rounds at most (R10).

====================================================================
PART 5. WRITE THE PIN COPY
====================================================================

Title, 60 to 100 characters: the search phrase first, then the hook. Specific, a little flirty. Example: "Cozy Fall Outfit Ideas for Moms: The Sweater and Boots Look for Every School Run".
Description, 450 to 500 characters including the disclosure: start with "#ad ", then a first sentence that repeats the search phrase naturally. Name what's in the look (by description, never brand). One line of personality in my VOICE from MY_RECIPE.txt. Then "Every piece is linked in my [Idea List / shop page]." Then 3 to 5 keyword hashtags (the #ad doesn't count). Then the DISCLOSURE LINE, last.
Alt text, 150 to 200 characters: a plain description of what's literally in the image, including the title words if they're on it.
Board: the board in MY_RECIPE.txt that fits the look. If the board has sections, use the one MY_RECIPE.txt names for that kind of look; if none fits, no section.
No price. No brand. No em dashes. No promise about what the buyer will get or how fast.
Write each pin's full copy into pin-drafts.md the moment it's finished.

====================================================================
PART 6. PUBLISH AND VERIFY
====================================================================

1. Confirm the destination URL is live and belongs to the look. If it does not resolve, stop this Pin.
2. Build the final draft record for each Pin:
   pin_id, look_id, image_id, title, description, alt_text, destination_url, board_name, board_url, board_id when available, scheduled_at, publisher, customer_selected, customer_selection_at, customer_schedule_authorized, schedule_authorization_at, schedule_authorization_fingerprint, status.
3. Set the Pin to customer_review_required. Show the customer the finished image and complete metadata. Do not route or schedule it until the customer selects this exact Pin, the exact public board is verified through an authorized supported path, the required disclosure is present, the destination resolves, all metadata matches, and the customer explicitly authorizes this Pin's schedule.
4. TIME-SAVING PATH:
   Use the connected Metricool workflow recorded in MY_RECIPE.txt only when PUBLISHING TEST = passed and the per-Pin approval gate is recorded.
   - Confirm the correct Pinterest brand/profile and exact board/section.
   - Create/schedule only the specifically selected and authorized Pin with the final image, title, description, destination URL, board and approved time.
   - Record the returned Metricool post identifier and read back the scheduled record when supported.
   - If the connected ChatGPT + Metricool workflow requires a separate platform approval, set awaiting_approval and report the exact step. Do not claim the Pin is scheduled or published before evidence confirms that state.
   - If the workflow cannot safely perform the action, leave it unscheduled and report the blocker.
5. CREDIT-SAVING PATH:
   Prepare the complete Pin package after customer selection and schedule authorization; set state to customer_scheduling_required. The customer performs the final scheduling action in Pinterest. Do not simulate it with browser automation.
6. Set state to scheduled only after the selected publishing layer confirms the Pin exists. Otherwise retain customer_review_required, customer_scheduling_required, or awaiting_approval as appropriate.
7. Verification:
   - TIME-SAVING: read the scheduled post back through Metricool when supported and compare image, title, board, destination, time and status.
   - CREDIT-SAVING: the customer confirms the Pin appears in Pinterest's scheduled area.
8. Never use Pinterest's undocumented internal endpoints or automated scraping to verify Pins. Never report a Pin as verified based only on an unconfirmed request.

====================================================================
PART 7. STATE, LEFTOVERS AND RECOVERY
====================================================================

1. Every completed Pin is written to structured state immediately. The human-readable pin-tab.md and storefront-log.md are updated from that state.
2. A run that stops early leaves completed assets and records intact. The next run reconciles existing LOOK_ID and PIN_ID values before creating anything new.
3. A repeated run must never create a second Pin for the same PIN_ID. If a publisher post ID already exists, reconcile it instead of creating another post.
4. Leftovers are processed before new looks:
   - queued or awaiting_approval Pins are resumed first
   - scheduled but unverified Pins are checked through the selected publishing layer
   - failed Pins are retried only when the failure is retryable
   - dropped Pins are never silently resurrected
5. Pull handling:
   - A scheduled/unpublished Pin is cancelled through the selected publishing layer.
   - An already-published Pin must be removed from Pinterest itself. Do not claim that deleting a Metricool record removes an already-published Pinterest Pin.
   - Record the exact final state: pull_requested, pulled, or pull_failed.
6. Associates-only path: the destination page must load before publishing. If it does not, the Pin remains queued.
7. Influencer path: if an Idea List is pending or unavailable, the Pin remains queued and no alternate look/list is substituted.
8. Image lost: rebuild from the logged product sheet and regenerate once. If the asset cannot be recovered safely, mark the Pin dropped and explain why.
9. Never silently substitute a new product, destination, board or image after a partial failure.
10. At the end of every run, write:
   - run_id
   - task
   - started_at
   - finished_at
   - result
   - created IDs
   - failed IDs
   - retryable IDs
11. On renewal date, do not schedule new looks beyond the renewal date. Tell the customer to renew the calendar.
On the RENEWAL DATE in MY_RECIPE.txt: schedule nothing past it, and tell me in one line that the calendar needs renewing (paste 02_SETUP_PROMPT.txt again and answer "renew").
