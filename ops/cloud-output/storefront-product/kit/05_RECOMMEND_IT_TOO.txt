RECOMMEND IT TOO (optional)
Putting The Brand Closet™ on your own pages.

[KIT BUILD NOTE, removed before this file ships: this file is written two ways. The desktop finish checks whether Brand Closet™ members get their own referral link, keeps VERSION A if they do and VERSION B if they don't, and deletes the other version and this note.]

====================================================================
VERSION A (members get their own referral link)
====================================================================

If you're a member of The Brand Closet™, you can recommend it with your own referral link, so a friend who joins through you is tracked to you. Rose's program sets the terms, and she can change them, so read them on her site before you promise anyone anything. I don't quote a rate here on purpose.

FIND YOUR LINK
1. Open The Brand Closet™ on Skool, signed in.
2. Click the group name at the top left to open the group menu, and choose the affiliate option (on Skool it's labelled Affiliates). Copy your link.
3. Paste it into MY_RECIPE.txt under a new line: BRAND CLOSET REFERRAL LINK: (your link), and change BRAND CLOSET CARD: off to on.

ADD A CARD TO YOUR SHOP-THE-LOOK PAGES (needs the blog half, 09_THE_BLOG_HALF.txt)
Paste this into a Claude chat with your website's code open:

------------------------------------------------------------------
Add a small card component to my shop-the-look section and show it on the hub page, every category page and every look page, just above the Amazon disclosure. Match my site's existing card style. The card has:
- a small label: "Love these looks?"
- a title: "Where my outfit ideas come from"
- one line: "The Brand Closet™ is Rose's community on Skool. It's free to join. The Outfit of the Day is on her $9/month tier, and she also has a $19/month tier. Her prices, her community."
- a button reading "Join The Brand Closet™", linked to my referral link from MY_RECIPE.txt, opening in a new tab, with rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"
- directly under the button, in small text: "Affiliate link: I earn a commission if you upgrade, at no extra cost to you."
The link, the rel attribute and the disclosure ship together or not at all. No commission rate anywhere. No em dashes. Name only what's written above; don't describe anything else Rose offers. Build the site, open one look page locally and show me the card.
------------------------------------------------------------------

ADD ONE LINE TO YOUR PIN DESCRIPTIONS (optional)
Pinterest descriptions can't hold a clickable link, so this line is awareness only; your card is where your link lives. If you want it, add to MY_RECIPE.txt: PIN LINE: on. The tasks then add this sentence before the disclosure on Outfit of the Day pins only, and still keep the description at 450 to 500 characters:
"Outfit ideas like this come from a styling community I'm in: search The Brand Closet™ on Skool."
