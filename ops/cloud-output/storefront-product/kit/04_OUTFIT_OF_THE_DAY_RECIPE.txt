THE BRAND CLOSET™ OUTFIT OF THE DAY PATH
LAUNCH STATUS: PERMISSION-GATED, NOT CORE

WHY THE HOLD EXISTS
The Brand Closet™ does release daily Outfit of the Day content. The frequency is not the problem.

The issue is commercial rights to paid member content. Skool's current Terms and Transaction Terms describe paid content/member access as personal and non-commercial and restrict commercial exploitation/copying of service content. A community post telling members to create/share images does not automatically grant a commercial license broad enough for an automation product.

Therefore:
- Daily OOTD availability is acknowledged.
- The current launch product does NOT automate from paid OOTD lessons.
- The path can be reactivated only after written permission/license from the content owner explicitly covering the intended commercial derivative workflow.

WHAT PERMISSION NEEDS TO COVER
Get written permission that clearly covers, at minimum:
1. access to paid OOTD lessons for this use
2. extraction of outfit/product attributes
3. rewriting or transforming lesson prompts
4. generation of derivative styling images
5. commercial publication on Pinterest
6. affiliate monetization
7. automated processing
8. use for your paid product/customer workflow
9. duration/territory and revocation terms

PUBLIC DAILY CONTENT IS NOT THE SAME THING AS A COMMERCIAL LICENSE.

★ MANUAL/LOW-COST PATH
Even with permission, a customer can use the OOTD content manually instead of paying for an automated connector workflow. The steps are the same: identify the outfit, document the product attributes in your own words, source your own links, create your own images and publish them through the approved Pinterest path.

WHAT THIS PATH NEVER DOES
- never copy Rose's lesson text
- never reuse Rose's paid images
- never upload Rose's paid images to an AI generator
- never use Benable links as the customer's affiliate links
- never assume "share it everywhere" overrides platform terms
- never activate the automation without written permission on file
