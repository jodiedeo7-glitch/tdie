====================================================================
THE RULES THAT MAKE THIS SAFE (never move)
====================================================================

B1. Rose's content is a reference only. Her flat lay images, her lifestyle images, her prompts and her lesson text are paid member content. Nothing of hers is ever posted, uploaded anywhere public, attached to a pin, attached to an image generator, saved to the computer, resold or shared. You look at her flat lay to know what the outfit is. If you screenshot it to see it better, that screenshot stays inside this run only: never saved to the folder, never placed next to the product sheet, never attached to anything.
B2. Benable links are never used. They pay their owner. Never open, click or copy them, and never use the product names, brand names or ASINs written in them (or anywhere in the lesson text) to search Amazon. Every piece is re-found on Amazon from your own plain-words piece list and linked with MY OWN SiteStripe short link (on this automation, captured for every piece whatever the path, themed-look recipe Part 3 step 3).
B3. My own list is the pin link. Influencer path: my own Idea List for the outfit. Associates-only path: my own shop page for the outfit (08_STOREFRONT_PATHS.txt).
B4. Brand cues are stripped from every prompt. Rose's lifestyle prompt may give the scene idea only: the moment and the mood (for example "grabbing a coffee on a crisp fall morning"). Rewrite that idea in your own words and set it in one of the places in PERSONA WORLD. Remove every brand name, store name, chain, mall, logo, designer reference, device brand, cup size or menu item, recognizable shop or landmark, and anything that names or imitates a real business. Take nothing else from her prompt: not the styling, not the format, not the wording.
B5. Own posting slots. This automation only uses its own two PIN slots in MY_RECIPE.txt (defaults: pin 1 at 4:30 pm, pin 2 at 9:30 am; on the persona path pin 2 is the lifestyle photo). They never collide with automation 1, and no two pins on the account sit within 1 hour of each other.
B6. My images are new images. Every flat lay and lifestyle photo is generated fresh from the Amazon pieces I found, using the product sheet of MY pieces (themed-look recipe, Part 3 step 6).

====================================================================
THE NIGHTLY RUN
====================================================================

0. Take the browser lock (R16).
1. Read storefront-log.md and pin-drafts.md. Schedule any Outfit of the Day pin logged "built" or "scheduled, not verified" first (themed-look recipe, Part 7 step 4).
2. Open The Brand Closet™ in one fresh tab. Go to the Classroom, open the "Outfit of the Day Closet" course, and list its outfit lessons posted today or in the 13 days before (the date shown on the lesson; if none shows, the date you first saw it). If the course isn't there, is locked, asks for an upgrade, or Skool asks you to sign in, stop and tell me in one line: "I can't open the Outfit of the Day course in The Brand Closet™: check you're signed in to Skool and still on the $9/month tier." If a course of Outfit of the Day lessons exists under a new name, use it and tell me the new name.
3. Pick the lessons whose address is NOT in the "Outfit of the Day lessons" table, oldest first, up to OUTFITS PER NIGHT (default 2). A lesson already in the table as "sourcing" or "built" is finished, not restarted: reuse its logged pieces and list link. If more than 4 lessons are new (a first run, or after a break), log all but the newest 4 as "skipped" with the note "backlog". If there are none and nothing is left over, close the tab, release the browser lock and end: "No new outfits tonight." Only say that after you've actually seen the lesson list.
4. For each lesson: add it to the table now, status "sourcing", with its address. Look at Rose's flat lay (B1). Write down each piece in plain words: type, color, cut, material, details. Pieces are what she wears: clothes, shoes, bag, jewelry, hair pieces, sunglasses if worn. Props (drinks, phones, flowers, books, candles) are not pieces. Fewer than 4 pieces? Add shoes, a bag or jewelry in the outfit's colors until there are 4. More than 8? Keep the 8 that define it. Don't open the Benable links (B2). Close the Skool tab before you open Amazon or any image tool.
5. Re-find every piece on Amazon with the sourcing rules in the themed-look recipe, Part 3: in stock, 4.0 stars or better, 100+ ratings, Prime where possible, closest match in type, color and cut, searched from your plain-words list. Capture my SiteStripe short link for every piece. No close match? Choose the nearest honest alternative and note it in the log. If a look from the last 30 days in storefront-log.md has the same hero piece, still build this outfit, but keep both of its pins at least 3 days from that look's pins.
6. Build my own list or page (B3). Title it for search: the outfit described in plain words, never Rose's lesson title (for example "Pink Color Block Hoodie Outfit"). Log the list link on the lesson's row.
7. Make pin 1, the flat lay or collage, in Seedream 4.5 on Higgsfield, from the product sheet of MY Amazon pieces (themed-look recipe, Part 4, Format A or B).
8. Make pin 2:
   PERSONA PATH: the lifestyle photo in Gemini (07_PERSONA_PATHS.txt prompt), set in a PERSONA WORLD place, with at most the moment and mood from Rose's lifestyle prompt, rewritten and stripped (B4).
   NO-PERSONA PATH: the other collage format, as in the themed-look recipe.
9. Write the pin copy (themed-look recipe, Part 5) into pin-drafts.md. Nothing from Rose's lesson text is copied, and Rose is never named. The Brand Closet™ is never named on a pin unless MY_RECIPE.txt says PIN LINE: on, and then only as the one sentence in 05_RECOMMEND_IT_TOO.txt. Set the lesson's row to "built".
10. Schedule (themed-look recipe, Part 6): pin 1 at the pin 1 slot on the day after the lesson date. If that slot has passed, is less than 1 hour away or is taken, use the first free pin 1 slot at least 1 hour from now. Pin 2 goes at the pin 2 slot 3 days after pin 1. If pin 2 would land more than 14 days ahead, schedule pin 1 only and log pin 2 "built"; a later run schedules it. Blank twice? Stop (rule R2) and let tomorrow night's run pick up the leftovers.
11. Verify on Pinterest's scheduled pins page, then log: set the lesson's row to "verified", update the look row, add both pins to pin-tab.md.
12. Release the browser lock. One-line report.

WHAT THIS AUTOMATION NEVER DOES
Post, upload, save or attach Rose's images. Use or search from Benable links. Copy her prompts or lesson text. Name Rose on a pin. Promise anyone a tier they aren't on.
