THE RULES THAT MAKE THIS SAFE (never move)
====================================================================

B1. Rose's content is a reference only. Her flat lay images, her lifestyle images, her prompts and her lesson text are paid member content. Her flat lay is captured as a screenshot into the working session only: never saved to my computer, never posted, never uploaded anywhere public, never attached to a pin, never attached to an image generator, never resold or shared. You look at it to know what the outfit is.
B2. Benable links are never used. The "Link to Outfit" button and every Benable link pay their owner. Never open, click or copy them, and never use the product names, brand names or ASINs in them (or anywhere in the lesson text) to search Amazon. Every piece is sourced through the authorized product-intake path in the themed-look recipe, Part 3, and linked with the customer's authorized affiliate link when the selected Amazon path requires one.
B3. My own list is the pin link. Influencer path: my own Idea List for the outfit. Associates-only path: my own shop page for the outfit (08_STOREFRONT_PATHS.txt).
B4. Brand cues are stripped from every prompt. Rose's lifestyle prompt may give the scene idea only: the moment and the mood (for example "grabbing a coffee on a crisp fall morning"). Rewrite it in your own words, for my persona, and set it in one of the places in PERSONA WORLD. Strip every brand cue: monograms, store names, red carts, logos, chains, malls, designer references, device brands, cup sizes and menu items, recognizable shops or landmarks, and anything that names or imitates a real business. Take nothing else from her prompt.
B5. Own posting slots. This automation only uses its own two PIN slots in MY_RECIPE.txt (defaults: pin 1, the flat lay, at 4:30 pm; pin 2, the lifestyle pin, at 9:30 am). They never collide with automation 1, and no two pins on the account sit less than 1 hour apart.
B6. My images are new images. Every flat lay and lifestyle photo is generated fresh from the Amazon pieces I found, using the product sheet of MY pieces (themed-look recipe, Part 3 step 6).
B7. The two pins of one outfit are never on the same day. The flat lay goes out the day after the outfit's date; the lifestyle pin at least 3 days after the flat lay; nothing is ever scheduled more than 14 days ahead.
B8. Public board first. Ask the customer to confirm public status after checking the exact board in Pinterest's official interface; record the board URL, check method and time. Resolve its stable identifier only through an authorized supported publisher path. Never guess an ID or use hidden endpoints/scraping. If public status or exact board assignment cannot be verified, schedule nothing and report the board blocker. A TEST board is for a dry run only; a dry run never creates or schedules a Pin.

====================================================================
THE NIGHTLY RUN
====================================================================

0. Create a RUN_ID and claim the run in structured state before doing production work. If an active run already owns the same work window, reconcile it instead of starting a second run. Work out when this run must be out: 30 minutes before the next RUN time in MY_RECIPE.txt SLOTS, or 30 minutes before my shutdown time, whichever comes first.
1. Read storefront-log.md, pin-drafts.md and structured state. Reconcile existing records first. A Pin logged "built" or "scheduled, not verified" is not permission to schedule: leave it in customer_review_required until the customer selects that exact Pin and explicitly authorizes its schedule after all checks pass.
2. Open the course in one fresh supported browser session: https://www.skool.com/the-brand-closet/classroom/272075b5
   Use the rendered classroom/course interface and the information exposed by the supported browser session to locate the recent lessons. Do not depend on Skool's private JavaScript objects, __NEXT_DATA__, undocumented endpoints, or DOM implementation details.
   If the course isn't there, is locked, asks for an upgrade, or Skool asks you to sign in, stop and tell me in one line: "I can't open the Outfit of the Day course in The Brand Closet™: check you're signed in to Skool and still on the $9/month tier."
3. Read the Outfit of the Day lessons table and storefront-log.md before selecting new work. Resume any lesson already logged "sourcing" first. Then pick every not-yet-completed lesson dated within the last 14 days (by its date title or updatedAt), oldest first. Do not silently discard backlog: if an unstarted lesson is older than 14 days, log it as missed and report it. If there are no eligible or in-progress lessons, close the tab and end: "No new outfits tonight." Only say that after you've actually read the lesson list and state.
4. Before starting each new outfit, check the time available until the run limit. Use the documented estimate in MY_RECIPE.txt; if none is set, use the current estimate of about 2 hours per outfit. If it cannot be finished, leave it queued for the next run without changing its status. The 14-day eligibility window is a recovery limit, not permission to lose work silently.
5. For each lesson: add it to the table now, status "sourcing", with its id and title. Open it with the course address plus ?md=<lesson id> and read its page text. Take one screenshot of Rose's flat lay into this session only (B1). Write down each piece in plain words: type, color, cut, material, details. Pieces are what she wears: clothes, shoes, bag, jewelry, hair pieces, sunglasses if worn. Props (drinks, phones, flowers, books, candles) are not pieces. Fewer than 4 pieces? Add shoes, a bag or jewelry in the outfit's colors until there are 4. More than 8? Keep the 8 that define it. Note the lesson's lifestyle prompt only for its moment and mood (B4). Don't touch the "Link to Outfit" button (B2). Close the Skool tab before you open Amazon or any image tool.
6. Source every Amazon piece through the current authorized product-intake path in 03_THEMED_LOOK_RECIPE.txt, Part 3. Default: use customer-supplied ASIN/product rows. Advanced: use the customer's authorized Amazon Creators API integration if available. Never browse Amazon through simulated human interaction, never capture affiliate links through browser automation, never invent an ASIN or affiliate link, and never use Benable links as a product source. If a required product row is missing, set the lesson to sourcing/intake_needed and tell the customer exactly what is missing. If a look from the last 30 days in storefront-log.md has the same hero piece, still build this outfit, but keep both of its pins at least 3 days from that look's pins.
7. Build my own list or page (B3). Title it for search: the outfit described in plain words, never Rose's lesson title (for example "Pink Color Block Hoodie Outfit"). Log the list link on the lesson's row.
8. Read 13_PIN_VISUAL_STANDARD.md and make pin 1 using the configured provider and model in MY_RECIPE.txt, following 03_THEMED_LOOK_RECIPE.txt, Part 4. Apply its full-frame, no-generated-text and product-fidelity acceptance checks. Supply authorized product facts as prompt text; do not attach a text product sheet. Add headline text after generation with the approved deterministic layout when available. Do not hard-code a provider or ask the image model to render exact typography.
9. Make pin 2:
   PERSONA PATH: create the lifestyle image with the configured available provider/model and the prompt in 07_PERSONA_PATHS.txt, set in a PERSONA WORLD place, using only the moment and mood from Rose's lifestyle prompt after rewriting and stripping it (B4).
   NO-PERSONA PATH: use the alternate collage composition in the themed-look recipe, Part 4, with the configured provider/model.
10. Write the pin copy (themed-look recipe, Part 5) into pin-drafts.md. Nothing from Rose's lesson text is copied, and Rose is never named. The Brand Closet™ is never named on a pin unless MY_RECIPE.txt says PIN LINE: on, and then only as the one sentence in 05_RECOMMEND_IT_TOO.txt. Set the lesson's row to "built".
11. Propose schedule times (themed-look recipe, Part 6; public-board check B8): pin 1 at the pin 1 slot on the day after the outfit's date. If that slot has passed, is less than 3 hours away or is taken, propose the first free pin 1 slot at least 3 hours from now. Pin 2 goes at the pin 2 slot at least 3 days after pin 1 (B7), within the 14-day storefront limit. If pin 2 falls outside that window, keep it as a draft and report the reason. Store proposed times, but keep both Pins in customer_review_required until the customer selects each exact Pin and explicitly authorizes its schedule. Only then use the supported publisher path. A blank page or unavailable action stops the run; preserve completed records and report the blocker. Do not promise automatic catch-up.
12. Read back scheduled Pins through the selected supported publisher when available. Otherwise ask the customer to confirm Pinterest's scheduled area. Set state to scheduled only when that evidence confirms the Pin is queued there; use verified only after supported evidence or customer confirmation is recorded. Update the lesson and look records and pin-tab.md with the actual result.
13. Write this run into structured state first, then refresh the Runs view in storefront-log.md (task "nightly", date, started, finished, result). One-line report.

Save, post, upload or attach Rose's images. Use Benable or other third-party links as product destinations. Copy her prompts or lesson text. Name Rose on a Pin. Schedule before per-Pin customer selection and explicit schedule authorization. Claim publication or verification without evidence. Promise anyone a tier they aren't on.
