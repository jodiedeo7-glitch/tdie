THE RULES THAT MAKE THIS SAFE (never move)
====================================================================

B1. Rose's content is a reference only. Her flat lay images, her lifestyle images, her prompts and her lesson text are paid member content. Her flat lay is captured as a screenshot into the working session only: never saved to my computer, never posted, never uploaded anywhere public, never attached to a pin, never attached to an image generator, never resold or shared. You look at it to know what the outfit is.
B2. Benable links are never used. The "Link to Outfit" button and every Benable link pay their owner. Never open, click or copy them, and never use the product names, brand names or ASINs in them (or anywhere in the lesson text) to search Amazon. Every piece is sourced through the authorized product-intake path in the themed-look recipe, Part 3, and linked with the customer's authorized affiliate link when the selected Amazon path requires one.
B3. My own list is the pin link. Influencer path: my own Idea List for the outfit. Associates-only path: my own shop page for the outfit (08_STOREFRONT_PATHS.txt).
B4. Brand cues are stripped from every prompt. Rose's lifestyle prompt may give the scene idea only: the moment and the mood (for example "grabbing a coffee on a crisp fall morning"). Rewrite it in your own words, for my persona, and set it in one of the places in PERSONA WORLD. Strip every brand cue: monograms, store names, red carts, logos, chains, malls, designer references, device brands, cup sizes and menu items, recognizable shops or landmarks, and anything that names or imitates a real business. Take nothing else from her prompt.
B5. Own posting slots. This automation only uses its own two PIN slots in MY_RECIPE.txt (defaults: pin 1, the flat lay, at 4:30 pm; pin 2, the lifestyle pin, at 9:30 am). They never collide with automation 1, and no two pins on the account sit less than 1 hour apart.
B6. My images are new images. Every flat lay and lifestyle photo is generated fresh from the Amazon pieces I found, using authorized descriptions of MY pieces as prompt text (themed-look recipe, Part 3). A text product sheet is not an image attachment.
B7. The two pins of one outfit are never on the same day. The flat lay goes out the day after the outfit's date; the lifestyle pin at least 3 days after the flat lay; nothing is ever scheduled more than 14 days ahead.
B8. Public board first. Ask the customer to confirm public status after checking the exact board in Pinterest's official interface; record the board URL, check method and time. Resolve its stable identifier only through an authorized supported publisher path. Never guess an ID or use hidden endpoints/scraping. If public status or exact board assignment cannot be verified, schedule nothing and report the board blocker. A TEST board is for a dry run only; a dry run never creates or schedules a Pin.
