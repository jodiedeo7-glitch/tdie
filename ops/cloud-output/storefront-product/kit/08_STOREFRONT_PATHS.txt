STOREFRONT AND DESTINATION PATHS

AMAZON ASSOCIATES
Associates provides the affiliate-link program. Use your own compliant Special Links and an approved publishing destination.

AMAZON INFLUENCER
Influencer is part of Associates with additional eligibility and approval. Use the storefront/content features actually enabled on your account. The kit supports your existing eligible public Idea List without requiring a website.

CURRENT CAPABILITY, NOT A BLANKET LABEL
Amazon's April 14, 2026 policy change notice describes Storefront and unique-link features for creators more broadly. Do not claim storefronts can only ever belong to Influencers. Do not promise all Associates can create Influencer content or earn onsite commissions.
During setup inspect and record: Associates status, Influencer status, Storefront access, Idea List access and onsite eligibility as separate fields. Use UNKNOWN for capabilities that were not observed.

PATH A: EXISTING ELIGIBLE PUBLIC IDEA LIST
Create/maintain it yourself in Amazon and record its exact URL and selected ASINs.
Verify list title, contents and destination. An owner-session view does not prove signed-out public visibility; test that separately before marking it passed.
Low-cost Claude/ChatGPT: ask for a list title/description from your own selected products.
Manual alternative: write the title/description and maintain the list yourself.

PATH B: YOUR COMPLIANT LIVE WEBSITE
Create a page with original styling images, plain-language descriptions, your Special Links, affiliate and AI-image disclosure.
Low-cost Claude/ChatGPT: request one page draft using verified product rows.
Manual alternative: paste the page and links into your site's editor.
Time-saving alternative: a supported authorized site connector, followed by live readback.
Never schedule a Pin to an unpublished page.

OPTIONAL AMAZON/PINTEREST CONNECTION
Use eligible native product tagging only if the connection is visibly available on your account. Read its exact affiliate destination and displayed disclosure. Do not assume it replaces the Idea List, #ad or account disclosure.
Low-cost/manual: configure and use the native controls yourself.

DISCLOSURE FOR BOTH PROGRAM PATHS
Opening affiliate disclosure: #ad.
Ending kit statement: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
Use that statement prominently on the linked social account/profile as Amazon requires. Being an Influencer does not replace it with an unverified alternate statement.
Influencer status can be accurately named separately.

REGISTER SOURCES
Keep Amazon's website/social list current. Finish the save and read the registered list back. Never answer factual audience/compliance declarations by guess.

LINKS AND IMAGES
Use your own Special Links, written product attributes and rights-cleared image inputs.
Amazon-hosted images are not automatically licensed for AI transformation. Explain AI images as styling inspiration and avoid exact-product-photo claims.
Amazon's current rules permit some opt-in email/SMS/direct messaging use subject to their conditions. The kit may choose website-first email routing as a product convention; do not call all email affiliate links universally banned.

PUBLISHING
Native Pinterest: customer reviews/schedules the exact finished Pin.
Approved connector/API: only after account-specific testing and explicit individual approval.
Metricool records and native scheduled Pins are separate. Reconcile both.
