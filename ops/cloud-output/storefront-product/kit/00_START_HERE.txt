THE WHILE-YOU-SLEEP STOREFRONT™
Start here

Your Amazon product choices become Pinterest pins that go out on a schedule, pointing people who are already searching for the look straight to your Idea Lists (or your own shop-the-look pages).

You set it up once. After that, scheduled tasks in Claude build the downstream work: they take the products and affiliate links you provide, make the images, write the pin copy, schedule the pins and log everything. Product selection and Amazon-side actions are deliberately kept outside the automated agent path. On the Influencer path, you still create the Idea List yourself, then the machine takes over. On an Associates-only site path, you may also paste a generated page yourself. 08_STOREFRONT_PATHS.txt explains the two paths.

Two things you should know before you start, because I'd rather you hear them now than find out on day three:

1. Scheduled tasks are created in Claude and the schedule itself runs in Claude's service. Because this kit works with a local Storefront folder and your browser, the Claude Desktop app must be open and connected when a task needs those local resources or browser controls. The pins themselves are scheduled in Pinterest, so your computer does not need to be on when the Pin goes live.
2. Every Pin the tasks prepare is written to your pin tab and your log before it is scheduled. You can review the queue and type PULL to remove one before it posts, or delete it yourself in Pinterest. The product keeps this existing pull-before-post workflow.

  2. Open the Setup Guide PDF and follow the numbered steps. It tells you exactly when to paste each file.
  3. That's it. The PDF is written for someone who has never scheduled a task in her life.

HOW LONG IT TAKES

Setup is designed to be short once your accounts are ready. The recurring build is downstream of your product intake: you choose the products and create the destination list or page, then the task handles image creation, copy, scheduling and logging. That boundary is intentional.

What it does NOT promise: sales. Pinterest is search. Pins get found over weeks and months, not hours, and nobody can tell you when a pin will get its first click. This kit builds and runs the machine. What people buy is up to them and Amazon.
