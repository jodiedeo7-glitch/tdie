THE WHILE-YOU-SLEEP STOREFRONT™
Start here

Your approved Amazon product inputs become reviewable Pinterest Pin drafts. You select each Pin and authorize its schedule.

The storefront now has two publishing paths:

TIME-SAVING
ChatGPT Work builds the look, images and Pin package, then uses the connected Metricool publishing layer when that connection is available and tested.

CREDIT-SAVING
ChatGPT Work builds everything. You perform the final scheduling action through Pinterest's native scheduler.

The product is intentionally built so you do not have to pay for maximum automation if you would rather save money.

IMPORTANT: the old desktop-browser architecture is retired from the customer instructions. The computer-on/browser workflow is now a LOCAL fallback, not the default.

WHAT YOU NEED

1. An eligible ChatGPT account with access to Work and Scheduled Tasks.
2. Amazon Associates, plus Amazon Influencer approval if you want Idea Lists.
3. A Pinterest business account with public boards.
4. Higgsfield if you choose the Higgsfield image path.
5. Metricool if you choose TIME-SAVING publishing.
6. Google Drive if you choose CLOUD execution/state.
7. The Brand Closet™ only if you choose the optional Outfit of the Day automation.

THE TWO MODES

CLOUD
Preferred for the true while-you-sleep experience. ChatGPT Work runs remotely and uses supported connected apps. Your computer does not need to remain on.

LOCAL
Fallback when a local folder, local browser session or unsupported site requires your computer. ChatGPT Work runs locally in the desktop app.

PUBLISHING

TIME-SAVING:
ChatGPT Work prepares a Pin draft. The connected Metricool path may schedule it only after the customer selects that exact Pin, confirms the destination and public board, checks disclosure and metadata, and explicitly authorizes that schedule. Connected actions and board assignment must be available and verified for the customer's account.

IMPORTANT:
Do not schedule a Pin automatically from a recurring task. A setup test may schedule only the exact Pin the customer selected and explicitly authorized. Record publisher readback when supported; approval requests or unavailable board assignment leave the Pin unscheduled and must be reported.

CREDIT-SAVING:
ChatGPT prepares the complete Pin package and the customer schedules it in Pinterest.

Pinterest's native business scheduler currently supports standard Pins up to 30 days ahead and up to 10 future Pins at once.
