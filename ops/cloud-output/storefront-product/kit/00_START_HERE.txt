THE WHILE-YOU-SLEEP STOREFRONT™
Start here

Your Amazon links, turned into Pinterest pins that go out on a schedule, pointing people who are already searching for the look straight to your Idea Lists (or your own shop-the-look pages).

You set it up once. After that, scheduled tasks in the Claude desktop app do the building: they pick the look, find the pieces on Amazon, grab your affiliate links, make the images, write the pin copy, schedule the pins and log everything. On the Amazon Influencer path, you don't build, you don't sell, you don't post. (One path has hand-work: Associates only, on a website the tasks can't publish to, you paste each look's page in yourself, about 10 to 15 minutes a look. 08_STOREFRONT_PATHS.txt explains.)

Two things you should know before you start, because I'd rather you hear them now than find out on day three:

1. The tasks run from your own computer. It needs to be switched on, with Chrome open and signed in to Amazon, Pinterest, your image tools and (for the Outfit of the Day) Skool, at the times the tasks run. If the computer is off, that run waits until it's back on. The pins themselves post from Pinterest, so your computer doesn't need to be on when they go live.
2. Nothing is ever posted without leaving a trail. Every pin the tasks schedule is written to your pin tab and your log. Glancing at them is optional. It's also how you pull anything you don't like before it goes live: type PULL next to it by 11:30 am on its posting day, or delete it yourself in the Pinterest app any time.

THE ORDER

  1. Read 01_REQUIREMENTS.txt. If something on it is missing, get it first.
  2. Open the Setup Guide PDF and follow the numbered steps. It tells you exactly when to paste each file.
  3. That's it. The PDF is written for someone who has never scheduled a task in her life.

HOW LONG IT TAKES

Setup is about 5 minutes of your time once your accounts are in place: you paste one prompt and answer six questions (a couple have a quick follow-up). The runs themselves take longer, and a build keeps Chrome busy for a while (plan on up to an hour a look while it's new), but it's the task doing the work, not you.

What it does NOT promise: sales. Pinterest is search. Pins get found over weeks and months, not hours, and nobody can tell you when a pin will get its first click. This kit builds and runs the machine. What people buy is up to them and Amazon.
