REQUIREMENTS
Read this before anything else. If one of these is missing, the machine doesn't run, and I'd rather you know now.

WHAT YOU NEED FOR BOTH AUTOMATIONS

1. A Claude plan that includes scheduled tasks and Claude in Chrome.
   Both have to be on your plan. Plans and what they include change, so check on claude.ai before you buy anything.

2. The Claude desktop app, on a computer that is switched on when the tasks run.
   The tasks run from your own computer. It stays on, with Chrome open and signed in, at the times you choose. If it's off at run time, the daily missed-run sweep (9:45 am) runs the missed build once when the computer is back on. You pick run times that suit your life during setup.

3. Google Chrome, signed in to everything below, with the Claude in Chrome extension installed in that one browser only. If the extension is connected in two browsers (say Chrome and Edge), a scheduled task can't choose between them and falls back to the Claude desktop app's own browser, which may not be signed in. Remove it from the other browser, or also sign in to Amazon, Pinterest, Higgsfield and Skool in the Claude desktop app's browser.
   The tasks never type a password. If you're signed out of something, the task stops and tells you in one line.

4. Amazon Associates.
   You need an approved Associates account so every link carries your tag. You'll see the SiteStripe bar across the top of Amazon when you're signed in: that's how the tasks capture your links.
   PLUS Amazon Influencer approval if you want Idea Lists (the best link for a pin, and hands-off with no website needed). Amazon approves Influencers from certain social accounts; check its current Influencer page. No Influencer approval? The Associates-only path in 08_STOREFRONT_PATHS.txt needs a website, and on most site builders you'll paste each look's page in by hand.
   Amazon needs every place your links appear listed in your Associates account (your Pinterest profile, and your website if you have one). New accounts: check Associates Central for Amazon's current first-sales rule, because pins take time.

5. A Pinterest business account with public boards.
   Secret boards reach nobody. The recipe checks every board is public before the first pin, and stops if one isn't. (I learned this one the hard way: one of my own boards sat on secret while pins were scheduled to it.)

6. Image tools:
   Higgsfield makes every flat lay and collage (no person in them). There are two ways to run it, and it's a real trade-off. Your setup chat asks which one you want.
   A. FREE IMAGES, MORE CLAUDE USAGE (the website). The tasks open the Higgsfield website in Chrome and use a model on your plan's Unlimited list, with the Unlimited switch on (next to Generate). Images cost no Higgsfield credits. But driving a website in Chrome uses noticeably more of your Claude usage and keeps Chrome busy longer. Best free model in my tests: Seedream 5.0 Lite (backup: Seedream 4.5). On 3 Oct 2026 the Unlimited list came with Higgsfield's Plus plan, $49/month (Higgsfield's price, not mine; plans change, so check their page).
   B. LESS CLAUDE USAGE, A SMALL CREDIT COST (the plugin). The tasks use the Higgsfield plugin inside Claude instead of the website. It's faster and much lighter on your Claude usage, but the plugin can't use the Unlimited switch, so every image costs credits. Best value in my tests: Seedream 5.0 Flash, half a credit an image at full size, so about 1 credit a look (two pins). Close second: GPT Image 2.5 Flare on low quality, also half a credit, slightly smaller. Skip the cheapest model, Soul 2.0 (about an eighth of a credit): it's made for people and can't spell a title.
   How I tested: the same flat lay prompt and the same product sheet through every model, judged on four things: every piece there and right, the title spelled right, nothing invented (no stray computer monitor), nothing cut off at the edge. Seedream 4.5 doubled one piece and added a monitor; GPT Image (the original) cut a piece off the edge and doubled the latte; FLUX.2 Pro left pieces out; Nano Banana and Nano Banana 2 came out too small; Kling O1 Image swapped in things that weren't on the list. Nano Banana Pro was the best of all, at 2 credits an image.
   Get Higgsfield through my link: https://higgsfield.ai?fpr=jodie-30b092
   Affiliate link: I earn a commission if you subscribe, at no extra cost to you. Higgsfield's own offer for people who join through a link was, when I wrote this, up to 50% off for three hours after you log in through it (their terms, check them on the page).
   Persona photos (persona path only) are made on Higgsfield too: Nano Banana Pro, about 2 credits a photo, from her image saved in Higgsfield once at setup. A scheduled task can't upload a file from your computer, which is why it's saved there first. No persona? Skip this (see 07_PERSONA_PATHS.txt).

7. Only with the blog half (09_THE_BLOG_HALF.txt): Chrome signed in to GitHub, with write access to your site's repository.

WHAT YOU DON'T NEED

  No website on the Influencer path (the Associates-only path and the blog half need one).
  No Instagram. It isn't part of the core machine.
  No design skills, no coding, no face on camera.

WHAT THIS COSTS YOU IN TIME

About 5 minutes to start once the accounts above are in place. After that: nothing on the Influencer path. On the Associates-only path with a site the tasks can't publish to, about 10 to 15 minutes a look to paste its page in. The optional glance at your pin tab and log is how you pull a pin you don't like.

While a build runs, leave the Chrome window it's using alone, and set your computer not to sleep at run times. Each look keeps the task busy for a while (plan on up to an hour a look while it's new), and more looks a week means more of your Claude usage.

THE HONEST PART

Automating Amazon and Pinterest carries some account risk. The kit is built to go slow and stop at the first sign of trouble (it reads pages the way a person would, never scrapes in the background, waits between pins, and stops after two blank pages), and you run it on your own accounts, at your own call.

This kit runs the machine. It does not promise sales. Pinterest is a search engine: pins get found over weeks and months. Every pin carries your #ad disclosure, and no price ever appears on a pin, because Amazon prices change by the hour.
