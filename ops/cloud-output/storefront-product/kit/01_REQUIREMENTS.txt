Read 14_AUTOMATIC_EXECUTION_CONTRACT.txt first. Its main-path and capability rules apply throughout this file.

REQUIREMENTS

Read this before you buy anything else. Most of it you only need for the time-saving path.

====================================================================
EVERYONE NEEDS
====================================================================

1. AMAZON
An approved Amazon Associates account.
For the Idea List path, also Amazon Influencer approval and a storefront. No Influencer approval? The Associates-only path uses a page on your own website instead (08_STOREFRONT_PATHS.txt).
On AUTOMATIC, the agent sources products and records links belonging to your account through the permitted route tested at setup. It never invents products, ASINs or tags. Creators API is one eligible catalog route, not a requirement assumed for every buyer. Browser account actions require supported access and applicable permission; see 14_AUTOMATIC_EXECUTION_CONTRACT.txt. On the optional manual route, you supply your own product rows and links.

2. PINTEREST
A Pinterest business account with at least one PUBLIC board. A secret board gets zero reach.
Pinterest's own scheduler holds standard Pins up to 30 days ahead, and up to 10 scheduled at once. The kit never schedules more than 14 days ahead.

3. AN AI CHAT
Any ChatGPT or Claude plan for the setup interview, the copy and the image prompts.

====================================================================
ONLY FOR THE TIME-SAVING PATH
====================================================================

4. CHATGPT WORK
The automated path runs as ChatGPT Work scheduled tasks. Work is available on eligible paid ChatGPT plans, and what's switched on can vary by account, region and workspace. Ordinary Scheduled Tasks on their own are not the full automation.
Cloud runs with your computer off only if every required account action has passed in that runtime. Local browser execution requires your computer and app available. Test the entire route, including Amazon and destination writes, before choosing.
No Work or equivalent recurring runtime? Setup reports the unavailable automatic capability and preserves the work. You may choose the optional credit-saving path; it is never substituted silently.

5. AN IMAGE TOOL
Higgsfield is the tested connected image tool. Its ChatGPT connection needs an active paid Higgsfield subscription. Setup records the exact model, its cost per image and your commercial-use rights, and never switches model or spends quietly.
Credit-saving alternative: AI writes the prompt and you run it yourself in the image tool you choose. You can also use your own photos or images you're licensed to use.

6. METRICOOL
Schedules your Pins for you. Connects to ChatGPT through Metricool's own app.
Metricool's Free plan has a monthly limit on published posts (20 when this kit was written). The default pace of 4 looks a week is about 34 Pins a month, so plan on a paid Metricool plan for this path, or lower your pace.

7. GOOGLE DRIVE
For CLOUD mode, your kit files and records live in one Google Drive folder that ChatGPT can read and write.

====================================================================
ONLY FOR THE OPTIONAL EXTRAS
====================================================================

8. THE BRAND CLOSET™: only for the Outfit of the Day automation. You need access to its Outfit of the Day lessons. Check the current tiers on its page before upgrading. To run it as a scheduled task, your ChatGPT mode must be able to open your signed-in Skool classroom; setup tests this for you. If access fails, keep that optional automation queued. You may choose to describe each lesson yourself as a manual alternative.
9. YOUR OWN WEBSITE: any website for the Associates-only path. A site on GitHub only for the blog half (09_THE_BLOG_HALF.txt).
10. INSTAGRAM: only for the Instagram add-on.

====================================================================
WHAT YOU DON'T NEED
====================================================================

No single browser-agent brand is mandatory. Automatic browser-only steps do require a supported, permitted operator.
No Pinterest developer app.
No Amazon API.
No website on the Influencer path.
No Instagram for the core machine.
No design skills, no code, no face on camera.

====================================================================
TWO HONEST NOTES
====================================================================

Automating Amazon and Pinterest carries some account risk. The kit is built to go slowly, never scrape or fake clicks, and stop at the first sign of trouble. You run it on your own accounts, at your own call.

Never type a password into a task or a chat. Sign in through each service's own sign-in screen.
