REQUIREMENTS
Choose your operating mode before buying tools.

CREDIT-SAVING / LOW-COST
Use the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already have for short planning and copy requests, subject to your account limits. No paid scheduler, connector or developer application is required.
Manual alternative: choose products yourself, write the copy yourself, use a rights-cleared photo or original layout, and create/schedule the Pin in Pinterest's native composer.
Optional image generation uses only your chosen provider's verified commercial-use rights. Do not buy a plan on the assumption that the founder's Unlimited access transfers to you.

TIME-SAVING / MIXED
A supported scheduled runtime may prepare images, copy and state through connected tools. Record whether it runs locally or in the cloud and prove that it reads and writes the same persistent state during an actual run.
A local run needs its host awake and available. A cloud run can use only the connections/files available to that cloud runtime. Never assume a local folder is available there.
Metricool is optional. Verify the exact Pinterest provider, public board ID, image, destination, title, alt text, approval and schedule by reading the existing post back.
Use a supported approved publisher only after an account-specific test. Missing capabilities mean BUILD/QUEUE ONLY and manual Pinterest scheduling.
No automatic catch-up or unattended publishing is promised before it is tested on the customer's account.

AMAZON ASSOCIATES AND INFLUENCER
The Influencer Program is part of Associates and has additional eligibility/approval. It adds influencer storefront/content features and a dedicated onsite identity where applicable. Your Influencer status does not replace the Associates disclosure requirements.
Amazon's April 14, 2026 change notice also describes Storefront/unique-link features for creators more broadly. Do not promise that every Associates account has every Influencer feature, or tell an Associates-only customer that no storefront can ever exist. Inspect the actual account capabilities.
Use an existing public eligible Idea List/Storefront destination when your account supports it. Otherwise use your own compliant live website page with your own Special Links.
For new applications, verify Amazon's accepted traffic sources; do not promise Pinterest-only application eligibility.
Register the actual sites/social profiles where your Special Links appear. An entered URL is not verified saved until Amazon confirms the final save.

AMAZON INPUT
Choose products, ASINs, Special Links and Idea Lists manually, or use an independently eligible approved integration within its permitted scope. The default agent reads customer-supplied PRODUCT_SOURCES.md and never invents missing data.
Use written product attributes, your own photographs, or explicitly licensed images with transformation/commercial rights. Do not assume Amazon-hosted product photos or paid classroom images are licensed AI inputs.
Every Pin begins with a clear affiliate disclosure such as #ad and ends with: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
Associate is the program disclosure; Influencer is your additional account status. Identify both accurately in your account information. Put the required statement prominently on the linked social profile/account as Amazon requires.

PINTEREST
Use a business account and public production board. Record exact board/section names and IDs. Verify the destination URL before scheduling.
Each finished Pin needs its own explicit approval of image, copy, destination, board and intended date/time. Withdrawn dates are HOLD, never replacements.
Keep native Pinterest records separate from Metricool. A missing post in Metricool does not prove a native scheduled Pin is missing.
Use applicable AI-content/person labels. AI styling images must be described as inspiration and must not imply exact product photography.

BACKUP AND STATE
Keep MY_RECIPE.txt, PRODUCT_SOURCES.md, storefront-log.md, pin-tab.md and pin-drafts.md in a confirmed writable location available to the selected runtime. Keep a local backup.
Production state is separate from test files and from general TDIE automation.
Record unknowns as UNKNOWN. Never convert a historical test result into a current pass.

OPTIONAL PATHS
Persona: optional; use only a reference with consent and rights.
Blog: optional; verify publishing access and live page before linking a Pin.
Brand Closet OOTD: optional, disabled until content-use rights and any access requirements are verified.
Instagram: separate optional workflow; no earnings claims on Facebook or Instagram.

Start with one prepared draft and read it back. Do not buy the automation stack or enable recurring publication until the exact account workflow passes.
