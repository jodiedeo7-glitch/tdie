INSTAGRAM ADD-ON (optional)
Instagram is not part of the core machine. The While-You-Sleep Storefront™ is Pinterest, your Amazon lists and (if you add it) your own site. Everything below is extra, and one part of it is done by hand.


CREDIT-SAVING / MANUAL ALTERNATIVE
Do not require connected scheduling or an additional paid plan. ChatGPT or Claude may prepare the caption and a ready-to-post asset from the customer's approved look. The customer can create and schedule the feed post in their own supported Instagram/Meta interface, or publish manually; Stories remain manual. Record account, asset, disclosure, AI label and final publishing confirmation. Do not treat a prepared file as scheduled or published. No earnings or income claims.

WHAT CAN RUN ON ITS OWN
Feed posts. If your Instagram account is a professional account connected to a Facebook Page you manage, Meta Business Suite can schedule feed posts, and a task can load them for you. Per look: a carousel with pin 2 first and pin 1 second, and a caption that names the pieces (by description, never brand), carries #ad, and ends "every piece is linked in my shop (link in bio)".

WHAT IS DONE BY HAND
Instagram stories are posted by hand. Instagram's website can't post stories, and link stickers only work from the app. If you want a story per look with a "shop this look" link sticker, the task can save the story image and the link into your folder, and you post it from your phone. That's hand-work, which is exactly why it isn't in the core machine.

THE RULES
- No links anywhere on Instagram: not in captions, comments or replies. Calls to action point to your link in bio. (Story link stickers are the one exception, and those are by hand.)
- #ad on every post with an affiliate look.
- No earnings or income figures on Instagram, ever.
- Before sharing, read the account name at the top of the post composer. It must be the account you mean to post from. Switch accounts if it isn't.
- Turn on Instagram's AI label for every AI-made image.
- Your link in bio: your /lifestyle page if you have the blog half, otherwise your Amazon storefront.

TO TURN IT ON
Add to MY_RECIPE.txt: INSTAGRAM: feed posts on, account (your handle), posting time (for example 7:00 pm). Then add this line to the end of the JOB section of your weekly task: "If MY_RECIPE.txt says INSTAGRAM: feed posts on, follow 10_INSTAGRAM_ADD_ON.txt for each look after its pins are verified." If Business Suite isn't connected for your account, the task writes each post into your folder as a ready-to-post file instead, and tells you.
