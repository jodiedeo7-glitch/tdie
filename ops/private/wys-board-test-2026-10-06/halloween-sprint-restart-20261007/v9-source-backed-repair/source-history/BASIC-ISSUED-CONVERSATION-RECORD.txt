**The strongest difference is folded product edit versus expanded, dimensional outfit.** Across your runs, the open-cardigan-and-diagonal-jeans arrangement keeps repeating, making the two versions look alike.

Use these two replacement prompts with the **original outfit reference**, rather than a generated result. Both are saved, and the composition rule is pushed to the repo. These new outputs are still untested.

**Basic — compact, separated, exactly five items**

:::writing{variant="standard" id="63821"}
Create a realistic directly overhead BASIC outfit flatlay, portrait 2:3. Use the attached original outfit reference for product appearance, not its composition.

Show exactly FIVE products: pink textured-knit button cardigan, white ribbed cropped tank, blue wide-leg jeans, one matching pair of pink black-striped gum-sole sneakers, and cream gold-chain shoulder bag. Preserve their reference colors, materials, construction and hardware.

Create a compact, orderly product edit on one smooth warm-ivory cotton surface. Keep the products separate with clear gaps.

TOP ROW: button the cardigan and neatly fold it into a rectangle, sleeves underneath, with neckline and button placket visible. Place the white tank separately beside it.

MIDDLE ROW: fold the jeans into a compact rear-side-up rectangle, lower legs tucked underneath. Clearly show the genuine rear waistband, yoke and embroidered rear pockets.

BOTTOM ROW: arrange both sneakers together at left and the cream bag at right, with its chain gathered naturally beside it.

Nothing forms a dressed-body silhouette. Do not arrange the open cardigan around the tank. No long diagonal trouser leg. Keep every product recognizable, inside the frame and at believable relative scale.

Reserve the upper 15 percent for “off duty” in thick hot-pink brush script spanning approximately 75 percent of the image width. Underneath, place “THE PINK EDIT” in bold near-black uppercase sans serif. Make the lettering clearly readable on a phone and wholly above the products.

Soft window light from upper left, balanced exposure, tactile knit fibers, denim weave and gentle contact shadows.

Only the five shopping products. No jewelry, hair clip, sunglasses, watch, phone, flowers, drink, stationery or bedding border. No added bows or ribbons, furry surface, watermark, CGI shine, bloom or artificial distressing. Preserve the jeans’ existing embroidery exclusively on their actual rear pockets; never combine rear pockets with a front zipper-fly panel.
:::

**Styled — expanded outfit, visible volume, coordinated finishing accessories**

:::writing{variant="standard" id="63822"}
Create a realistic directly overhead STYLED outfit flatlay, portrait 2:3, showing Tommy Kate’s girly, slightly glittery everyday outfit prepared on her farmhouse bedroom bed. Use the attached original outfit reference for product appearance only; create a new composition.

Show the same FIVE shopping products: pink textured-knit button cardigan, white ribbed cropped tank, blue wide-leg jeans, one matching pair of pink black-striped gum-sole sneakers, and cream gold-chain shoulder bag. Preserve their actual colors, materials, cut, construction and hardware.

Make the OPEN cardigan and tank an expanded, dimensional centerpiece across the upper two-thirds of the composition. Layer the tank inside the cardigan as they would be worn. Extend one sleeve loosely outward and bend the other gently inward.

Concealed garment support creates unmistakable rounded shoulder and chest volume, tubular sleeves and deep empty cuff openings. Give the tank gently supported torso volume. The garments rest horizontally on the coverlet, with natural compression and grounded shadows. Preserve normal continuous cardigan shoulders.

No folded rectangular cardigan, product grid, visible person, skin, mannequin, stuffing, shoulder cutouts, floating fabric or exaggerated inflated shapes.

Place the jeans separately at lower right, rear-side up, loosely folded with one lower-leg section extending toward the bottom edge. Show their genuine rear waistband, yoke and existing embroidered rear pockets. Never put rear details on a front zipper-fly panel. Preserve the wide-leg cut and equal actual leg lengths.

Stagger the sneakers naturally at lower left. Position the cream bag beside the cardigan without concealing its sleeves. Keep every shopping product within the frame.

Finish the wearable outfit with a layered pearl necklace following the tank neckline, one matching pair of pearl studs grouped beside it, and a saturated hot-pink fine-glitter claw clip near the bag. These are the only extra accessories, at believable scale. No gaming equipment, watch, sunglasses, phone or random decorative filler.

Use a smooth ivory cotton coverlet with relaxed folds, a lavender sheet edge and a visible narrow pink-and-cream gingham border. Ivory dominates; the colored bedding frames the outfit, and the glitter clip adds a vivid hot-pink accent. No desk, work surface or furry textile.

Soft window light from upper left, balanced exposure, clearly dimensional fabric shadows, tactile knit and denim, subtle pearl luster and restrained gold reflections. Glitter consists of tiny physical reflective flecks.

No text, watermark, drink, flowers, notebook, pen, added bows or ribbons, hydrangeas, shag, artificial distressing, bloom or CGI finish. Preserve only the jeans’ existing embroidery in its correct rear location.
:::
