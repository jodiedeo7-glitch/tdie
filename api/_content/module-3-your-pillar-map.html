The idea

Publish straight from your inventory and you get a pile — every post an island, nothing linking, nothing compounding. Google can't tell what you're an authority on.

A pillar structure fixes that. Pick 8–12 big topics. Each gets one deep pillar article plus a cluster of supporting articles linking to it. Google reads the cluster and concludes you're an authority. Sixty unrelated posts never produce that signal, however good each one is.

Second reason, and the one that makes this a business: each pillar routes to an offer. One free resource that captures an email, one paid product. Set it once and every article in that pillar feeds the same two doors, permanently.

Do this

Group your inventory rows by topic until you have 8–12 clusters. Then fill this — one row per pillar:

# Pillar topic Pillar article title Free resource Paid product Related pillars Inventory pieces

The free resource must be genuinely free. Not "free with a $9 membership." No pillar has a $0 resource? Write NONE — build one. Putting a paid thing in a free slot converts worse than leaving it empty, because someone who clicks "free" and hits a paywall doesn't come back.

The paid product must already exist. Exact name and price from your Canon File. No match? Write GAP. Don't invent a product to fill a row and don't force an unrelated one in. GAP is a real answer — it's your product roadmap.

Related pillars: pick three. These become your internal links. Genuinely adjacent, not random.

Sequence them. Build first where you have the most inventory and a real paid product. You want your first build to be the one that works.

Roadblock — "I only have 4 or 5 topics"

Then you have 4 or 5 pillars. Five deep clusters outperform twelve thin ones every time. Add more as the business grows.

Roadblock — "Two pillars overlap"

Merge them. Two half-pillars chasing the same keywords split your own ranking signal and you get outranked by someone with one good page.

Roadblock — "Half my paid column says GAP"

Normal, and useful. A content exercise just produced a prioritized product roadmap. Build the site first — traffic tells you which gap to fill.

Do this — put it in project knowledge

Save the pillar map as pillar-map.md and upload it to your Claude Project, alongside the inventory from Module 1 and the Canon File from Module 2.

Then add one line to your Canon File so the two don't drift apart:

PILLARS — see pillar-map.md in project knowledge.
Free and paid slots for each pillar are defined there.

Your project now holds three files: what you own, what you sell, and how they map to each other. Every build prompt for the rest of the weekend reads all three automatically.

Deliverable

A pillar map, 8–12 rows, sequenced, gaps marked honestly — uploaded to project knowledge.
