5 · PRODUCT REGISTER

Every new product gets an entry before any assets are made

Copy this prompt
PRODUCT REGISTER ENTRY

Name:        [Full™ Name — exactly as it appears everywhere]
Price:       [exact, one figure]
Platform:    [where it's sold]
URL:         [live URL, or PENDING — do not invent]
Slot:        [which pillar / tier, or standalone off-ladder]
Audience:    [who specifically]
Format:      [what they receive]
Status:      [registered / live / retired]

The rule this enforces: name, price, and slot are decided before a single asset is created. Build assets first and you end up renaming a product across nine files.

6 · WEEKLY OPERATING CADENCE

Module 20 — 2–4 focused hours a day

Daily - 90 min — one thing that compounds (writing, SEO passes, building a resource). One task, no switching. - 30 min — circulation (pins, community, comments). Timeboxed, hard stop. - Not daily: analytics.

Weekly - One supporting article published - One repurposing pack run on it - 30-min review, same time each week: what published, what's queued, what's blocked

Fortnightly - One pillar article

Monthly - Search Console — what are you appearing for that you didn't target? - Test your own email signup end to end - Check bridge page destinations

Quarterly - Verify affiliate products still exist at the published price - Broken link audit - Pillar performance review — which pull, which have empty paid slots - Run the cut list

The cut list - Pillar under 3 supporting articles a year after launch → cut - Platform with six months of posting and no measurable traffic → cut - Product with no sales in two quarters and no strategic reason → cut

Never automated: final approval on anything published · community replies · anything involving a price or a claim.
