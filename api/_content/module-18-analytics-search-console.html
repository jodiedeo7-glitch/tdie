The idea

Two tools, different jobs. You need both and neither takes long.

Search Console tells you what happened in Google — what people searched, what you appeared for, what got clicked. It's the only place this data exists.

Site analytics tells you what happened on your site — which pages people read, where they came from, where they left.

Do this — analytics

If you're on Vercel, use Vercel Analytics. It's built in, it's free at your level, and it needs no cookie banner because it doesn't track individuals.

Add Vercel Web Analytics to my site. Add the package to
package.json and include the Analytics component in every layout
that renders its own <html> tag — check all of them, not just
the main one. Give me each changed file complete.

The trap: if any page has its own <html> instead of using a layout, analytics won't load there and that page reports zero traffic forever. Ask for every file, not the obvious one.

Then enable it in the Vercel dashboard — Project → Analytics → Enable. Code alone isn't enough.

Do this — Search Console

Set up in Module 7. Now use it. Two reports:

Performance — every search that showed your site, with impressions, clicks, and position. This is where your next articles come from.

Pages — what's indexed and what isn't, with reasons.

What actually matters in month one

Impressions. Not clicks — impressions. Impressions mean Google is showing you. Clicks follow, but impressions move first and they're the early signal that anything is working.

Which queries you appear for. Frequently not the ones you targeted. This is genuinely useful information: a query you're at position 15 for, that you never wrote about deliberately, is an article you should write on purpose.

Which pages get indexed. New pages take days to weeks. If something isn't indexed after a month, check it for a noindex tag or an orphaned URL with no internal links.

What does not matter in month one

Traffic totals, bounce rate, time on page, and every number a course sells you a dashboard for. Your sample size is too small for any of it to mean anything, and watching them daily is how people talk themselves into quitting a system that's working normally.

Check monthly. Not daily.

Roadblock — "Analytics shows zero"

Enabled in the dashboard? Snippet in every layout? Ad blocker on your own browser? Check in that order.

Roadblock — "Search Console shows nothing after two weeks"

Normal. New sites take weeks to accumulate enough data to display. Nothing to fix.

Deliverable

Analytics on every layout and enabled in the dashboard, Search Console verified with a submitted sitemap.
