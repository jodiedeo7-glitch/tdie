ASK-FOR-IT LIST

Forty-three things you can change on your site by asking for them in one sentence.

How to use this. Open a conversation in your Claude Project, paste the ask, and add the file path or page name if you know it. You don't have to know it — "the page with my pricing on it" works.

One rule that makes all of these work better. Claude can only change what it can see. Before any of these, either paste the file, or use the GitHub commit prompt from Module 20 so Claude can read your repo directly. If it comes back asking which file, that's why.

These are not the build prompts. Those live in the Prompt Vault. This is the list for after the site exists — the small asks you'll make every week for years.

A · LOOK AND FEEL

1 — Change a colour everywhere

Change my footer background to lavender across the whole site, and adjust the text colours so everything still reads properly against it.

The second half matters. Change a background without it and you get grey-on-lavender somewhere you didn't look.

2 — Change a font

Swap my body font to Inter across the site. Keep the display font as it is.

3 — Make something bigger or smaller

My headline is too big on desktop and too small on mobile. Scale it so it moves smoothly between the two.

4 — Fix spacing that feels off

The gaps between my sections are inconsistent. Make them even down the whole page.

5 — Round or square the corners

Make every card and button on the site use 4px corners instead of fully rounded.

6 — Add a subtle hover effect

When someone hovers a product card, lift it slightly and deepen the shadow. Keep it subtle.

7 — Change the whole page's mood

This page feels cold. Warm it up — softer background, warmer text colour, more generous line spacing. Don't change the layout.

8 — Rebuild one section

This section looks generic. Rebuild it in my brand — my palette, my fonts, no gradient boxes.

B · NEW PAGES

9 — A preview or sneak-peek page

Build me a public page that shows what's inside my course — the module list, what a module looks like, and what buyers get. No email gate. Link it from my sales page.

10 — A waitlist page

Build a waitlist page with a signup form that adds people to my [platform] list, and a version of the page that switches to "doors are open" on a date I set.

11 — A bridge page

Build me a page at /go/[name] for [product]. Real content about why I recommend it, an affiliate disclosure above the button, and rel="sponsored" on the link.

12 — A thank-you page

Build a page people land on after buying. Tell them what happens next and when to expect access.

13 — A resource landing page

Build a landing page for [freebie]. Headline, three bullets, one signup, nothing else on it.

14 — A page that lists something automatically

Build a page that lists every article in /learn automatically, reading the title and description from each file. I don't want to maintain a list by hand.

C · SOMETHING'S WRONG

15 — You can't name what's wrong

Something looks off on this page and I can't put my finger on it. Tell me what's wrong before you change anything.

This one is worth more than it looks. Ask it to diagnose first and you find out why, which stops it happening again.

16 — Invisible or unreadable text

The text on this button is invisible. Find out what's overriding it and fix it.

17 — Something overlapping

This banner is floating over my navigation when I scroll. Fix the positioning.

18 — A broken link

Find every link on my site pointing to [old URL] and change it to [new URL].

19 — The deploy failed

My build failed with this error: [paste it]. Fix it.

20 — A page looks different from the rest

This page doesn't match the others. Make it use the same layout everything else uses.

21 — Something changed and shouldn't have

This used to work and now it doesn't. Here's the file — what changed?

D · MOBILE

22 — Text running off the edge

Text is running off the screen on my phone. Fix the word wrapping across the whole site.

23 — A layout that breaks

This two-column section is squashed on mobile. Stack it instead below 760px.

24 — A sticky buy bar

Add a bar that sticks to the bottom of the screen on mobile once someone scrolls past the hero, with the product name, the price and a button. Hide it on desktop.

25 — Tap targets too small

My buttons are hard to tap on a phone. Make every clickable thing at least 44px tall on mobile.

E · CONTENT AND SEO

26 — A new article

Write a pillar article on [topic] using my Canon File for products, prices and voice. 3,000 words, chapter structure, answer-first paragraphs, an FAQ at the end. Then convert it into a page file and add it to my Library index.

27 — Expand something thin

This article is under my word floor. Expand it by adding depth, not padding — more specific examples, more of the actual how.

28 — Fix meta descriptions

Write a meta description under 155 characters for every page that's missing one. Make them earn the click, not describe the page.

29 — Internal links

Add internal links between these articles where it makes sense in context. Don't add a "read more" list at the bottom.

30 — Add schema markup

Add Article schema to my article layout, generated from the props. If a publish date is missing, leave it out — never invent one.

31 — Update a price everywhere

Change every mention of $67 to $97 across the whole site — buttons, product cards, notes, image alt text.

32 — Stop a page being indexed

Add noindex to this page so it stays out of search until I say otherwise.

F · IMAGES

33 — Put an image somewhere

Put this image in the header of that page, with proper alt text.

34 — A link preview image

Make me a link preview image for this page — the right size for social, with a headline over it — and set the page to use it.

35 — An image is cropping badly

My cover image has text baked into it and it's getting cut off. Stop it cropping.

36 — Images loading slowly

My images are too heavy. Compress everything in /images and tell me what each one dropped to.

37 — Add alt text

Every image on this page is missing alt text. Write it — describe what's actually in each image.

G · SELLING

38 — A countdown

Add a countdown banner to this page ending [date and time], that switches itself to "it's open" when it hits zero. I don't want to change anything manually on the day.

39 — A price ladder

Set my sales page up so I can change one line and it updates the price, the button text and the note everywhere at once.

40 — More buy buttons

This page only has a buy button at the top and bottom. Add them at the moments where someone is most likely to be convinced, each with a one-line reason above it.

41 — Make a product look like the flagship

My main product is a row among twenty others. Give it its own block at the top with the image, the key numbers and the price.

42 — An update log

Build a page that shows buyers everything I've updated since launch, plus a banner on the main page when there's something new.

H · LEGAL AND ADMIN

43 — A privacy policy

Build me a privacy policy page. I collect email addresses through [platform] and use [analytics tool]. Plain English, not boilerplate.

44 — Terms of purchase

Build me a terms page covering what buyers get, how access works, and my refund position, which is [state it].

45 — Cookie or analytics disclosure

Add a short line to my privacy policy explaining what my analytics tool collects.

46 — An affiliate disclosure

Add an affiliate disclosure above every outbound affiliate button on the site, in plain words.

Read anything legal before you publish it. Claude writes a good draft from what you tell it. It doesn't know your jurisdiction, your business structure, or your actual refund practice — you do.

I · THE ONES PEOPLE DON'T KNOW TO ASK FOR

47 — Ask what's wrong before asking for a fix

Read this page as a skeptical visitor who's never heard of me. Where would they stop reading? What wouldn't they believe? Don't fix it yet — just tell me.

The most valuable ask in this file. You'll get things you'd never have thought to request.

48 — Ask it to check its own work

You just changed four files. List every other place on the site that mentions the same thing and might now be out of date.

This catches the price you forgot on the homepage.

49 — Ask for the reason, not just the change

Fix it, then tell me why it broke so I know what to avoid next time.

50 — Ask it to refuse

If anything I've asked for conflicts with my Canon File, or with something we decided earlier, stop and flag it rather than guessing.

51 — Ask for the boring maintenance

Go through my whole site and find anything inconsistent — prices that don't match, old links, stale copy, alt text that's wrong. List it before changing anything.

Worth running once a month. It finds things you stopped seeing.

THE ONE THING THAT MAKES ALL OF THIS WORK

None of these asks work well on a site you don't control.

You can't ask for a countdown on a page a platform renders for you. You can't change a colour sitewide when the colour lives in someone else's theme. You can't add schema, or a bridge page, or a noindex tag, to a page you only rent.

That's the real argument for owning your site — not that it looks better, but that it's the only version where asking for something means getting it.
