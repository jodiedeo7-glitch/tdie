The idea

Every article you publish contains a month of content for every other platform. Extracting it by hand takes hours. Extracting it with one prompt takes minutes.

This is the module that makes the whole system sustainable. Without it you write an article and then face the second job of promoting it. With it, publishing and promoting are one action.

Do this — the repurposing pack prompt

Paste your published article, then:

Build the repurposing pack for this article.

LIVE FACTS:
- Article URL: [url]
- Free resource: [name] at [url] — or NONE
- Paid product: [exact name and price] at [url] — or GAP, omit
- Brand voice and banned words: see Canon File

Routing:
- Pins link to the ARTICLE
- Email and link-in-bio link to the RESOURCE PAGE

Produce:

1. TEN PINS. Each a distinct structure — no two alike. For each:
   - Title, written as a phrase someone would search
   - Description, 100-200 characters, keyword-rich, no hashtag spam
   - A complete image generation prompt, fully self-contained
   - Which board it goes on

2. INSTAGRAM CARDS — 8 slides, hook slide first, plus a caption
   and five hashtags that are all real search terms, no brand tags

3. TWO SHORT-VIDEO CONCEPTS — one 6-second loop, one action clip.
   Include a still cover image prompt for each.

4. THREADS / X — three posts, each standing alone

5. FACEBOOK — one post in a warmer, more personal register

6. EMAIL — subject line, preview text, body, one CTA to the
   resource page

7. COMMUNITY POST — warm and informal, not editorial voice

8. HEADLINES & SUBHEADS — a plain list of every headline used
   above, for reuse

Rules:
- Every asset self-contained. I will not assemble pieces.
- Image prompts complete and inline — never "use the prompt above."
- No invented products, prices, or links.
- No hype vocabulary.

Why "self-contained" is in the prompt

Without it you get a pack with a reference table at the top and assets that say "use style from Pin 3." You then spend forty minutes assembling prompts by hand, which is exactly the work you were trying to avoid.

Ask for complete and you get complete. It's a longer output; that's the point.

Do this — the register shift

One thing to change by hand: community posts should not sound like your blog.

Your articles are polished. Your community post is a person talking. Warm, direct, emoji if that's you. Same information, different register.

Posting editorial voice into your own community is the fastest way to make the room feel like a broadcast, and rooms that feel like broadcasts go quiet.

Roadblock — "All ten pins look the same"

Add: Each pin must use a genuinely different layout structure — vary text placement, image count, whether there's a photo at all, and headline treatment. List the structure name for each before its prompt. Naming the structure forces variation.

Roadblock — "This is a lot of content to schedule"

Don't post it all in a week. One article's pack is a month of pins. Schedule the ten pins across four weeks, one per platform per week for the rest.

Deliverable

One complete repurposing pack, scheduled out.
